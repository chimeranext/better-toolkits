# Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) — B2G

PPPs are long-term contracts where the private partner builds/operates an asset
or service that a government needs. They are a **B2G revenue model** distinct
from one-off tenders.

## When to consider a PPP

- Large, long-lived infrastructure/service need.
- Government lacks upfront capital (private finance).
- Revenue tied to availability/performance (not just construction).

## Key dimensions

- Risk allocation (design, build, finance, operate, maintain)
- Payment mechanism (availability payments, user fees, shadow tolls)
- Contract length and termination clauses
- Compliance/regulatory (firma digital, fianzas, transparency)
