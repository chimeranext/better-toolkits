# VR/AR Skill — Multi-Tool Reference

## Purpose

This skill provides access to Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) workflows — immersive content creation, XR interaction design, 3D scene optimization, and real-time rendering — across engines and frameworks (Unity, Unreal Engine, Bevy, WebXR for Three.js) for training, simulation, product visualization, and industrial digital twins.

## Capabilities

- **XR Scene Authoring**: Immersive environments and 3D content layout
- **Interaction Design**: Hand tracking, controllers, gaze, and spatial UI
- **Performance Optimization**: Frame-rate budgeting, LODs, and asset optimization
- **Cross-platform Target**: Headsets (Quest, SteamVR) and WebXR browsers
- **Twin Visualization**: Real-time immersion in industrial digital twins
- **Mixed Reality**: Anchoring and spatial mapping for AR

## Usage

This skill enables VR/AR workflows for agents operating across Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenCode, providing tool-agnostic guidance for immersive experience development.
