permission.wav — attribution

This file is derived from the GNOME Default Sounds alert theme, specifically
`/usr/share/sounds/gnome/default/alerts/hum.ogg` (GNOME, GPL/CC-licensed sound
theme, distributed with the system). It was converted to WAV (44.1 kHz, stereo)
and its volume was raised by +8.6 dB (max ≈ -1.0 dB) so it is clearly audible
as an authorization prompt.

Source (original, as of 2026-10-30):
  /usr/share/sounds/gnome/default/alerts/hum.ogg

SHA-256 (vendored wav): 4db53bf844f74602653d4c400ddd9d0f8f580ea8449657c4cb3816ccff36fd7d

It is used by `disk-cleanup.sh` `notify_sound()` so authorization prompts play
a soft, polite tone asking for permission — deterministic, vendored in this
repo, independent of any npm cache or system theme at runtime.
