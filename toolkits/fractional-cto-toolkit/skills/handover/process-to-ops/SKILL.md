Read and follow [`references/handover/process-to-ops/protocol.md`](../../../references/handover/process-to-ops/protocol.md) (protocol SSOT). Hand over the process standard, not just the documents: every SOP/playbook/runbook has a verified owner, a refresh cadence, and a test, and ownership transfers only on explicit acceptance.
