# PMBOK 8 domain overlay ↔ ISO 16326 / 16085

**Policy:** Local `PMBOK.pdf` in Downloads is the **5th edition (2013)** — not
normative here. This crosswalk summarizes a PMBOK **8**-shaped domain lens for
optional coverage checks. Do not vendor copyrighted PMI PDF text.

Use as an **overlay** after the 16326/16085 score — never as a replacement for
those outlines.

## Domains

| Domain | What “covered” means on an SPMP | Typical 16326 / 16085 home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance | Decision rights, approvals, escalation, plan change control | Cover + §4 org/interfaces + §6 control + approval record |
| Scope | In/out of baseline, deliverables, acceptance | §1.2 + §4.5 + §7 |
| Schedule | Milestones, activities, dependencies, cadence | §5 planning + §6 reporting |
| Finance | Budget, cost constraints, commercial assumptions | §5 resources/budget + constraints |
| Stakeholders | Who is informed/consulted/approves; external parties | §4 interfaces + §8 communication |
| Resources | People, skills, client-furnished items, tooling | §4.6 + §5 staffing/resources |
| Risk | Strategy, profile, analysis, treatment, monitoring | §8 risk + **16085** RM plan / treatment plan |

## How to apply

1. Complete the 16326 checklist and 16085 risk checklist first.
2. For each domain, mark Presente / Parcial / Ausente / N-A with evidence.
3. Do not invent process areas from PMBOK 5 knowledge areas; stay on these seven domains.
