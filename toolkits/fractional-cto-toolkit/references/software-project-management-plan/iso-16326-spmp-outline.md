# ISO/IEC/IEEE 16326:2019 — SPMP content checklist

Normative outline for Software/Systems Project Management Plan content (Clause 7
family as used in practice). Cite clause numbers; do not paste standard text.

Use with `templates/iso-16326-spmp.md`. Every required section that has no content
must state N/A **and** the reason.

## Risk bridge

16326 requires risk management content inside the SPMP (or by reference). The
default companion outline is ISO/IEC/IEEE 16085:2021 §8.1.2 — see
`templates/iso-16085-risk-management-plan.md`. That companion may live inline under
§8.x or as a linked document; either way, audit scores it with the 16085 rubric.
