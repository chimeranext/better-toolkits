# Skill `rnd-alignment` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Ensure research & development investment serves business goals. Maps each R&D
initiative to a business objective, evaluates pipeline health through a
stage-gate view, and surfaces misaligned or orphan research for a decision.

Grounded in the CTO-role references: MIT CTO Program (R&D strategy, innovation
pipeline, stage-gate), Devox (Visionary).

## When to use

- Reviewing the R&D/innovation pipeline.
- Challenging whether R&D spend maps to strategic objectives.
- Prioritizing between exploration bets and delivery commitments.

## Method

1. **Inventory R&D initiatives** — in progress and planned (prototypes,
   experiments, feasibility studies, platform investments).
2. **Map each initiative to a business objective** with the expected outcome and
   the KPI that would prove it. If it maps to no objective, it is orphan
   research: flag it for a decision (re-scope, park, or cut).
3. **Evaluate pipeline health with a stage-gate view** (idea → feasibility →
   build → scale). For each gate record:
   - How many initiatives are at each stage.
   - Which ones are stuck (overdue at a gate).
   - The kill/gate criteria and whether they are applied.
4. **Identify misalignments:** initiatives whose KPI is not owned, bets that
   duplicate other work, and gaps where a strategic objective has no R&D
   support.
5. **Recommend reallocation:** re-scope misaligned work, gate stuck items, and
   propose where freed capacity should go.

## Artifact

An alignment matrix (initiative → objective → expected outcome → KPI → stage)
plus an R&D pipeline scorecard with stuck items and reallocation
recommendations.

## Contract

- R&D spend maps to a business objective; orphan research is surfaced, never
  silently continued.
- Gate decisions are explicit and recorded, with the criteria used.
- The output feeds the roadmap and portfolio skills for reallocation.
