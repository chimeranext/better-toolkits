# Skill `saudi-arabia-grc` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Determine and support the applicable Saudi Arabia compliance regime for data
protection and cybersecurity. The key instruments are the **Personal Data
Protection Law (PDPL)** and the **National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)**
standards.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as benchmark
only).

## When to use

- A client processes personal data of individuals in Saudi Arabia.
- A client operates in Saudi Arabia or serves the Saudi market.
- A client is a critical or regulated entity subject to NCA controls.

## Method

1. **Determine PDPL applicability**: the PDPL (issued by Royal Decree M/148 in
   2021, amended, effective since 14 September 2023, with implementing
   regulations) applies to controllers processing personal data of
   individuals in Saudi Arabia, whether inside or outside the Kingdom.
2. **Map PDPL obligations**: lawful bases (including consent), data subject
   rights, the Data Protection Officer (where required), records, security
   measures, and **cross-border transfer restrictions** — personal data may
   only be transferred outside the Kingdom in the cases permitted by the law
   (e.g., with consent, contractual necessity, or under safeguards), and only
   where the transfer does not undermine the data's purpose or national
   security. Document each transfer.
3. **Administer the regulator**: the regulatory body is SDAIA (Saudi Data &
   AI Authority) — confirm the current regulator's guidance and any
   registration/notification duties.
4. **Cybersecurity baseline**: apply the NCA standards where the entity is
   in scope — the **Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC)** (and the
   Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls, CSCC, for critical infrastructure).
   Map the controls to the client's systems and produce a gap assessment.
5. **Sectoral rules**: financial (SAMA), health, telecom (CST), and other
   sector regulators impose additional obligations.
6. **Gap analysis and remediation plan** across the applicable instruments.

## Artifact

A Saudi Arabia GRC pack: applicability determination, PDPL obligations
mapping, transfer analysis, NCA control gap assessment, and a remediation
plan.

## Contract

- Cross-border transfer justifications are documented and confirmed with
   Saudi counsel; PDPL restrictions are applied strictly.
- NCA applicability depends on the entity type and sector — verify the
   relevant standards.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
  — mappings, gaps, open items.
- Verify against current PDPL implementing regulations and NCA/SDAIA
  guidance as it evolves.
