## Current state

Colombia has a **general data protection law in force**: Ley 1581 de 2012
(Ley de Protección de Datos Personales), with Decreto 1377 de 2013
(reglamentación) and later decrees on the SIC registry.

Authority:

- The **SIC** (Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio) is the data
  protection authority.
- The Constitution guarantees habeas data (Art. 15), and Ley 1266 de 2008
  covers financial/credit data (hábeas data financiero).

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- Ley 1581/2012 + Decreto 1377/2013 for general personal data processing.
- Ley 1266/2008 for financial and credit-report data.
- SIC guidelines and the registry of databases (Registro Nacional de Bases de
  Datos).
- Sectoral rules for health (e.g., Ley 1581 application, health regs) and
  telecom (CRC).

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Treat Ley 1581 as the baseline: authorization (consent), purpose
   limitation, data subject rights (access, correction, update, deletion —
   "ACUD"), and security duties.
2. Register databases with the SIC as required.
3. Implement privacy notices and a request-handling workflow.
4. Keep an eye on recent SIC regulatory activity and proposed reforms
   (including convergences with GDPR-style concepts).
