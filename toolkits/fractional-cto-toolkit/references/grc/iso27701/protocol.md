# Skill `iso27701` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Extend an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS into a Privacy Information Management System
(PIMS) per ISO/IEC 27701:2019. The fractional CTO frames privacy governance on
top of the security control set, producing PII-focused controls and records.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as
benchmark only).

## When to use

- The client already has (or wants) an ISMS and processes personal data.
- The client must demonstrate privacy controls to customers or regulators.
- A client processes PII and needs a structured record rather than ad-hoc
  privacy work.

## Method

1. **Scope the PIMS** as an extension of the existing ISMS scope.
2. **Identify the privacy roles**: controller, processor, and sub-processor
   relationships for each processing activity.
3. **Build a PII inventory / record of processing** (RoPA): categories of PII,
   purposes, retention, transfers, and legal bases.
4. **Privacy risk assessment**: apply the risk framework to PII processing —
   confidentiality, integrity, and availability plus privacy-specific harms
   (unlawful processing, unauthorized disclosure, excessive collection).
5. **Map controls**: ISO 27701 Annexes (A/B: PII-controller and PII-processor
   controls) to the ISMS Annex A, plus the ISO 27002 informational
   background. Annex the GDPR-relevant mappings (e.g., data subject rights,
   breach handling, DPA clauses).
6. **Gap analysis**: compare existing ISMS evidence against the PIMS control
   set; produce a remediation plan.
7. **Operate**: run the PIMS alongside the ISMS — internal audit, management
   review, and evidence collection.

## Artifact

A PIMS addendum to the ISMS dossier: PIMS scope, RoPA, privacy risk treatment
plan, control mapping, and gap assessment.

## Contract

- The PIMS builds on the ISMS; if no ISMS exists, scaffold the security base
  first.
- The RoPA is current and includes controller/processor roles and transfers.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral; no client names.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer ownership to the Chief Compliance
  Officer (CCO) with the RoPA, open items, and evidence locations.
- Verify mappings against the current edition of ISO 27701 before publication.
