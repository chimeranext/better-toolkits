# Skill `itar` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Assess and support compliance with the US International Traffic in Arms
Regulations (ITAR), which control defense articles, defense services, and
technical data on the United States Munitions List (USML). The work complements
EAR (dual-use) and is owned with trade counsel.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as benchmark
only).

## When to use

- A client manufactures, exports, or brokers defense articles or services.
- A client shares technical data with foreign persons (including employees) —
  deemed-export risk.
- A client is preparing for a U.S. government or audit review.

## Method

1. **Classify under the USML**: determine whether the item/technical data
   falls in a USML category (I–XXI); if not, check EAR for dual-use (see
   `ear`). This decision drives everything else.
2. **Registration**: confirm the entity is registered with the Directorate of
   Defense Trade Controls (DDTC); maintain registration status.
3. **Screen parties and transactions**: restricted party screening, proscribed
   countries, and prohibited end-use/end-user indicators.
4. **Determine licensing**: identify the required authorization — license
   (DSP-5 export, DSP-61 temporary export, DSP-73 temporary import, etc.) or
   an exemption (e.g., ITAR § 123/124 exemptions) and the conditions.
5. **Control technical data**: manage access controls for foreign persons
   (deemed exports), marking of technical data, and transmission safeguards.
6. **Build the compliance program**: appointed empowered official (per §
   120.25), compliance manual, training, recordkeeping (5 years), and
   voluntary-disclosure posture.
7. **Audit and improve**: periodic self-assessments and refresher training.

## Artifact

An ITAR compliance pack: USML classification record, DDTC registration status,
screening map, licensing/exemption determinations, and the compliance manual.

## Contract

- USML classification is documented and current; borderline items are
   confirmed with DDTC or trade counsel.
- The empowered official is named and the compliance manual is adopted.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
  — classification, registration, open items.
- Confirm licensing with DDTC before export; keep records per ITAR.
