## Current state

Panama has a **general data protection law in force**:

- Ley 81 de 2019 (Ley de Protección de Datos Personales), which entered into
  force in 2021.
- The authority is **ANTAI** (Autoridad Nacional de Transparencia y Acceso a
  la Información).

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- Ley 81/2019: general principles, legal bases (including consent and
  legitimate interest), data subject rights, security obligations, and
  international transfer rules (with adequacy-like restrictions).
- ANTAI guidance and the registry/notification duties for databases.
- Sectoral: financial (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá), health, and
  the special regimes in the Colon Free Zone and financial center.

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Treat Ley 81/2019 as the baseline: consent, rights handling, security
   measures, and transfer restrictions.
2. Verify whether database notification/registry duties apply.
3. Implement a rights-request workflow and breach-handling process.
4. Verify with local counsel whether recent ANTAI guidance changes duties.
