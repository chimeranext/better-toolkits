## Current state

Argentina has a **general data protection law in force**:

- **Ley 25.326** (2000) on protection of personal data, with Decreto 1558/2001
  (reglamentación).
- The authority is the **AAIP** (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública).
- Argentina holds an **EU adequacy decision** (Commission Decision
  2003/490/EC), one of the few countries with that status — relevant for
  cross-border data flows from the EU.

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- Ley 25.326: consent, sensitive-data rules, data subject rights (access,
  update, deletion — habeas data action under Art. 43 of the Constitution),
  security measures, and the National Registry of Data Banks (Registro
  Nacional de Bases de Datos) registration duties.
- AAIP Resolutions (e.g., on international transfers, security measures, and
  the registry).
- Sectoral: financial (BCRA), health, and employment.

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Treat Ley 25.326 as the baseline: consent, ARCO-style rights, security
   measures, and database registration.
2. Handle international transfers carefully — the EU adequacy status is an
   asset; verify transfer mechanisms under AAIP rules.
3. Implement a request-handling workflow and keep the registration current.
4. Monitor reform discussions modernizing the law toward GDPR alignment.
