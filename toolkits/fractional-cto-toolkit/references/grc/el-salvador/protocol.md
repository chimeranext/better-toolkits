## Current state

El Salvador has **no comprehensive general data protection law** in force.
Bills have been introduced in the Legislative Assembly but none has been
enacted.

What exists today:

- **Constitutional habeas data** (Constitución Política, Art. 2): a remedy
  to access and protect personal information.
- **Sectoral rules**:
  - Financial: banking secrecy and regulations of the Superintendencia del
    Sistema Financiero (SSF).
  - Health: health-data handling rules.
  - Telecom/consumer: SIGET (Superintendencia General de Electricidad y
    Telecomunicaciones) consumer rules and the consumer protection law.

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- For public-sector information, access-to-information rules (Instituto de
  Acceso a la Información Pública — IAIP) apply.
- For private-sector processing, in the absence of a general law, best
  practice (ISO 27701, GDPR benchmark) and sectoral regulators govern.

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Implement a baseline privacy program (records, legal bases, security,
   breach handling) regardless of the legal gap.
2. Apply sectoral rules where the client is regulated (financial, health,
   telecom).
3. Track legislative activity toward a general data protection law.
4. Treat multinational/client expectations as de facto GDPR-like.
