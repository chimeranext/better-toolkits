# Skill `hong-kong-pdpo` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Assess and support compliance with the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy)
Ordinance (PDPO — Cap. 486), enforced by the Privacy Commissioner for Personal
Data (PCPD). It applies to persons controlling the collection, holding,
processing, or use of the personal data of individuals in Hong Kong.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as benchmark
only).

## When to use

- A client processes personal data of Hong Kong individuals.
- A client runs direct marketing in Hong Kong.
- A client transfers personal data out of Hong Kong or to overseas
  processors.

## Method

1. **Map the six Data Protection Principles (DPPs)**:
   - DPP1 — collection: lawful and fair, notice of purpose, and necessity.
   - DPP2 — accuracy and retention: accurate, current, not kept longer than
     needed.
   - DPP3 — use: only for the stated purpose, or a directly related purpose
     (with consent otherwise).
   - DPP4 — security: appropriate technical and organizational measures.
   - DPP5 — openness: policies and practices available.
   - DPP6 — access and correction: honor subject access and correction
     requests.
2. **Assess direct-marketing controls**: where personal data is used for
   direct marketing, obtain the required consent (and the prescribed notice
   for using data for marketing), and honor the "opt-out" right (the 2012
   and 2021 amendments tightened these duties).
3. **Check cross-border transfers**: DPP3 restricts transfer to a place where
   the data will be protected to a standard comparable to the PDPO, or the
   data subject has consented, or an exemption applies; assess and document
   the route.
4. **Data breach management**: although the PDPO does not mandate a general
   breach-notification duty, the PCPD guidance expects notification of
   breaches with real risk of harm; follow the PCPD breach-handling framework
   and keep the regulator informed.
5. **Implement processes**: subject-access request workflow, consent records,
   retention schedule, and staff training.

## Artifact

A PDPO pack: DPP mapping, direct-marketing consent register, transfer
assessment, breach-handling flow, and SAR workflow.

## Contract

- Subject access requests are answered within the statutory timeframe (40
  days).
- Transfer assessments are documented; consent is evidenced.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
  — mapping, registers, open items.
- Track the PCPD's guidance and any legislative amendments (e.g., on data
  breach notification) as they evolve.
