# Skill `iso42001` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Establish or assess an AI Management System (AIMS) per ISO/IEC 42001:2023 —
the first certifiable international AI management standard. The fractional CTO
delivers the management framework: context, AI risk assessment, lifecycle
controls, and AI policy.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as benchmark
only), aligned with the AI governance posture of the EU AI Act (see
`eu-ai-act`).

## When to use

- A client develops, procures, or operates AI systems and wants structured
  governance.
- A client must demonstrate AI risk management to customers, insurers, or
  regulators.
- A client has an ISMS and wants to extend governance to AI.

## Method

1. **Scope the AIMS**: which AI systems (and AI system lifecycle stages) are
   in scope — design, development, deployment, use, monitoring, retirement.
2. **Context and interested parties**: stakeholders, internal/external issues,
   and the intended uses of the AI systems.
3. **AI policy and leadership**: document AI principles, objectives, and
   accountability, approved by management.
4. **AI risk assessment**: identify AI-specific risks (safety, bias, explainability,
   privacy, reliability, misuse, legal) and score them against acceptance
   criteria.
5. **AI risk treatment**: select controls from the ISO 42001 control set
   (Annex A): AI lifecycle, data, transparency, human oversight, and
   third-party AI components.
6. **Impact assessments**: perform the AI impact assessment for each in-scope
   system, capturing intended use, limitations, and safeguards.
7. **Operations and evidence**: run internal audits and management review;
   collect demonstration evidence (logs, testing, oversight records).

## Artifact

An AIMS dossier: scope, AI policy, AI inventory, AI risk register and treatment
plan, impact assessments, and a certification-readiness roadmap.

## Contract

- Work is scoped to the management system; certification audits are run by an
  accredited certification body.
- Every AI system in scope has an owner, an impact assessment, and defined
  human oversight.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
  — AI inventory, risk register, open items, evidence locations.
- Verify against the current ISO 42001 edition; align control mappings with the
  EU AI Act risk tiers where relevant.
