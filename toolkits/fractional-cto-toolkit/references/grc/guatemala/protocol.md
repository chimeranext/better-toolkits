## Current state

Guatemala has **no comprehensive general data protection law** in force.
A data protection bill has been proposed in Congress but has not been enacted.

What exists today:

- **Constitutional habeas data / personal information protection** (Constitución
  Política, Art. 31): access and correction of personal information held by
  public registries and entities.
- **Sectoral rules**:
  - Financial: banking secrecy and regulations of the Superintendencia de
    Bancos (SIB).
  - Health: health-data handling rules.
  - Consumer/telecom: regulatory rules of the Superintendencia de
    Telecomunicaciones (SIT) and consumer protection.

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- For the public sector, the habeas data remedy and access-to-information law
  (Ley de Acceso a la Información Pública) apply.
- For private-sector processing, in the absence of a general law, best
  practice (ISO 27701, GDPR benchmark) and sectoral regulators govern.

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Implement a baseline privacy program (records, legal bases, security,
   breach handling) regardless of the legal gap.
2. Apply sectoral rules where the client is regulated (financial, health,
   telecom).
3. Track the pending bill's progress and any new guidance.
4. Treat multinational/client expectations as de facto GDPR-like.
