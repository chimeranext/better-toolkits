## Current state

Mexico has a **general data protection law in force for the private sector**:

- Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los
  Particulares (LFPDPPP, 2010) and its Reglamento (2011).
- The authority is **INAI** (Instituto Nacional de Transparencia, Acceso a la
  Información y Protección de Datos Personales).
- Public-sector holders are governed by a separate law (Ley de Protección de
  Datos Personales en Posesión de Sujetos Obligados).

## Preliminary applicable frameworks

- LFPDPPP (private sector): consent, privacy notice (aviso de privacidad),
  data subject rights — ARCO (Acceso, Rectificación, Cancelación, Oposición),
  and transfer rules.
- Reglamento de la LFPDPPP: ARCO procedures and security requirements.
- INAI criteria and the mandatory privacy-notice structure.
- Sectoral: financial (CNBV), health, employment, telecom (IFT).

## What a fractional CTO should do

1. Treat the LFPDPPP as the baseline: aviso de privacidad, consent,
   ARCO workflow, and transfer registers.
2. Implement security measures proportional to the data sensitivity (the
   Reglamento sets a risk-based minimum).
3. Build an ARCO request-handling process and response records.
4. Monitor INAI resolutions and any reforms modernizing the law (converging
   with international standards).
