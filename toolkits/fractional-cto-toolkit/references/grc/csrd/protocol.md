# Skill `csrd` — protocol SSOT

## Purpose

Support compliance with the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive
(CSRD — Directive (EU) 2022/2464), which expands sustainability reporting via
the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). The fractional CTO's
value is the *process* and *data* enablement (materiality, data collection,
control, assurance readiness), not the accounting content itself.

Grounded in the CTO-role references (Splunk, Devox; Sushegaad GRC as benchmark
only).

## When to use

- A client is an EU in-scope undertaking (large, listed, or group) subject to
  CSRD.
- A client must assess double materiality and prepare ESRS disclosures.
- A client needs to structure ESG data collection for assurance.

## Method

1. **Confirm applicability and phase-in**: check the entity's size/listing
   status and the reporting year thresholds (large undertakings reporting for
   FY2024 → published 2025; listed SMEs later at the staggered dates).
2. **Double materiality assessment**: assess both dimensions — **impact
   materiality** (the undertaking's impacts on people and environment) and
   **financial materiality** (sustainability matters affecting the
   undertaking's financial position/performance). Document the thresholds and
   the involvement of stakeholders.
3. **Scope the ESRS disclosures**: map the applicable standards — cross-
   cutting (ESRS 1/2) and topical: environment (E1–E5), social (S1–S4), and
   governance (G1). Determine which datapoints are material for the client.
4. **Build the data pipeline**: identify sources for the required metrics
   (GHG emissions, workforce, governance) across the value chain; set owners
   and controls.
5. **Gap analysis**: compare current reporting and data availability against
   the required disclosures; produce a remediation plan.
6. **Assurance readiness**: prepare the evidence trail for the limited (and
   later reasonable) assurance engagement.
7. **Report and iterate**: integrate into the management report; run the
   annual cycle.

## Artifact

A CSRD pack: applicability analysis, double-materiality matrix, ESRS scope
map, data-pipeline design, gap assessment, and the assurance-ready evidence
index.

## Contract

- Materiality determinations are documented and reasoned — they drive the
  whole report.
- Every reported metric has an owner, a definition, and evidence.
- Vendor-neutral and client-neutral.
- Handover: on engagement end, transfer to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
  — materiality, scope, gaps, evidence.
- Verify against the current ESRS texts and the Commission's interpretation
  guidance; confirm the transposition status in the client's member state.
