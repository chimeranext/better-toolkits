# Business model: B2B

B2B = selling to **businesses** (companies). Long sales cycles, higher ACV,
multiple stakeholders, contract-based.

## What drives revenue here

- **Pipeline** (opportunities → proposals → signed) — `revenue-ops`
- **Forecast accuracy** — `forecast_accuracy_tracker.py`
- **GTM efficiency** — `gtm_efficiency_calculator.py` (Magic Number, Rule of 40, NDR)
- **Pricing/packaging** — `pricing-strategist`
- **Triage** of pipeline work — `revenue-ops-triage`

## Skills dominant in B2B

`revenue-ops`, `revenue-ops-triage`, `pricing-strategist`, `attribution-readout`.

## Typical metric targets (SaaS)

LTV:CAC > 3:1 · Magic Number > 0.75 · NDR > 110% · Rule of 40 > 40%.
See `references/gtm-efficiency-benchmarks.md`.
