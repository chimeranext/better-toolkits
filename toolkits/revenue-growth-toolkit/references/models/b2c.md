# Business model: B2C

B2C = selling to **consumers**. High volume, short cycles, low AOV, self-serve.

## What drives revenue here

- **Paid acquisition** — `media-plan`, `creative-testing`, `budget-pacing`, `kill-rules`
- **Funnel + flywheel** — `revenue-strategy` (AAARRR)
- **Referral/K-factor** — `referral_tracker.py`
- **Churn** — `churn_detector.py`, `cohort_analysis.py`
- **Organic social** — `social-publishing` (Publora MCP)
- **Analytics** — PostHog (funnels/retention)

## Skills dominant in B2C

`media-plan`, `creative-testing`, `revenue-strategy`, `social-publishing`, `attribution-readout`.

## Typical metrics

CAC payback < 12 mo · K-factor > 0.2 · LTV:CAC > 3:1 · D30 retention target.
