# /startup-lifecycle:investor-matching

Guide to finding and evaluating investors using a 5-axis framework.

## Audience

Founders who need to raise capital and don't know how to evaluate investors.

## 5-Axis Framework

| Axis | Key questions | Weight |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Stage fit** | Do they invest in your stage? (pre-seed, seed, Series A) | 25% |
| **Check size** | Does their check size match your raise? | 20% |
| **Thesis alignment** | Does their thesis match your industry/vertical? | 25% |
| **Geography** | Do they invest in your region? | 15% |
| **Value-add** | What do they bring besides capital? (network, expertise, brand) | 15% |

## Process

### Step 1 — Define your raise

- How much do you want to raise?
- What stage are you at?
- What type of investor are you looking for? (angels, VCs, strategic, impact)

### Step 2 — Create investor list

- Use Crunchbase, AngelList, YC Venture Network
- Filter by stage, check size, thesis, geography
- Goal: 50-100 investors in your pipeline

### Step 3 — Evaluate with 5-axis framework

| Investor | Stage fit | Check size | Thesis | Geography | Value-add | **Total** |
|----------|-----------|------------|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Investor A | 22/25 | 18/20 | 22/25 | 12/15 | 12/15 | **86/100** |
| Investor B | 18/25 | 15/20 | 20/25 | 10/15 | 10/15 | **73/100** |

### Step 4 — Outreach

- **Warm intro** > cold email > cold DM
- Ask for introductions from founders who raised before
- Use LinkedIn to connect with partners (not associates)

### Step 5 — Pitch meeting

- Prepare a 10-slide deck
- Practice the 3-minute pitch
- Have the financial model ready
