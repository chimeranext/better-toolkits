# /startup-lifecycle:cofounder-matching

Guide to finding and evaluating co-founders using a 6-axis framework.

## Audience

Founders who need a co-founder but don't know how to evaluate candidates.

## 6-Axis Framework

| Axis | Key questions | Weight |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Domain fit** | Do they understand the domain/problem? | 20% |
| **Skill complementarity** | Do their skills complement yours? | 25% |
| **Values alignment** | Do they share values and vision? | 20% |
| **Equity expectations** | Do they agree on the split? | 15% |
| **Time commitment** | Can they dedicate full-time? | 10% |
| **Track record** | Do they have relevant experience? | 10% |

## Process

### Step 1 — Define your profile

Before looking for a co-founder, define:
- **Your role**: What do you bring? (tech, business, design, etc.)
- **What you need**: What skills are you missing?
- **Your equity expectation**: 50/50? Other split?
- **Your commitment**: Full-time? Part-time?

### Step 2 — Find candidates

| Channel | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Y Combinator Co-Founder Matching** | [ycombinator.com/cofounder-matching](https://www.ycombinator.com/cofounder-matching) |
| **Founder Institute** | [fi.co](https://fi.co) — founder network |
| **LinkedIn** | Search by skills + location |
| **Events** | Startup meetups, hackathons, demo days |
| **Your network** | Friends, ex-colleagues, acquaintances |

### Step 3 — Evaluate candidates

Use the 6-axis framework to score each candidate:

| Candidate | Domain fit | Skill comp | Values | Equity | Time | Track | **Total** |
|-----------|------------|------------|--------|--------|------|-------|-----------|
| Candidate A | 18/20 | 22/25 | 18/20 | 12/15 | 8/10 | 8/10 | **86/100** |
| Candidate B | 15/20 | 20/25 | 16/20 | 10/15 | 6/10 | 7/10 | **74/100** |

### Step 4 — Equity conversation

**YC Rule**: 50/50 is the default. If you can't agree on an equal split, you probably shouldn't be co-founders.

**When NOT to do 50/50**:
- Co-founder joining late (12+ months after): 10-30%
- Very unequal capital: adjust, but consider loan or preferred stock
- 3+ co-founders: 33/33/33, with 2-5% CEO bonus

### Step 5 — Vesting agreement

**Standard**: 4 years + 1 year cliff.

- If a co-founder leaves before year 1: they get nothing
- After the cliff: 1/36 of remaining shares vest monthly

## FAST Agreement for advisors

If you need an **advisor** instead of a co-founder, use the FAST Agreement (Founder Advisor Standard Template):

- **What it is**: Standardized contract for advisors who receive equity instead of cash
- **Typical equity**: 0.1-1%
- **Vesting**: 2 years
- **Templates**: Available in EN/ES (see references)
