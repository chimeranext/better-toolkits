# /startup-lifecycle:vesting

Guide to vesting: schedules, cliff, acceleration, sweat equity.

## Audience

Founders who need to understand how vesting works for founders, employees, and advisors.

## Vesting 101

**Vesting** is the process of earning full ownership of an asset over a set period of time. For startups, vesting almost always refers to equity: you earn full ownership of your grant by staying with the company as it grows.

## Standard Vesting Schedule

### 4 years + 1 year cliff (YC standard)

| Milestone | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Year 1 (cliff)** | 25% of shares vest. If you leave before, you get nothing |
| **Year 2-4** | 1/36 of remaining shares vest monthly |
| **Year 4** | 100% vested |

### Numeric example

1,000 options with 4-year vest + 1-year cliff:

| Date | Options vested | Cumulative |
|------|----------------|------------|
| Year 1 (cliff) | 250 | 250 (25%) |
| Year 1 + 1 month | +8.33 | 258.33 |
| Year 2 | +250 | 508.33 |
| Year 3 | +250 | 758.33 |
| Year 4 | +250 | 1,008.33 (100%) |

## Types of Vesting

| Type | Description | When to use it |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Time-based** | Equity earned over time of service | Standard for startups |
| **Milestone-based** | Equity earned upon reaching specific milestones | Less common, for sales roles |
| **Hybrid** | Combination of time + milestone | Specific cases |

## Acceleration

### Single-Trigger Acceleration

- **What**: All unvested equity vests at once upon a single event (almost always an acquisition)
- **Disadvantage**: Acquirers dislike it. They want to retain key members post-acquisition.

### Double-Trigger Acceleration

- **What**: Vesting accelerates only if TWO events occur: (1) acquisition AND (2) involuntary termination (typically 9-18 months post-closing)
- **Advantage**: Acquirers prefer it because it preserves the requirement of ongoing service to vest.

## Sweat Equity

### What it is

Equity earned through time, skills, and effort instead of cash.

### For whom

| Role | Typical equity | Vesting |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Founders** | 20-50% each | 4 years + 1 year cliff |
| **Advisors** | 0.1-1% | 2 years |
| **Early employees** | 0.5-5% | 4 years + 1 year cliff |
| **Contractors** | Varies | By milestone or time |

### FAST Agreement (Founder Advisor Standard Template)

- Created by Founder Institute
- For advisors who provide connections, credibility, and advice
- Typically 0.1-1% equity with 2-year vesting

### Common mistakes

- **Giving too much equity**: Many founders give too much to advisors and don't get value in return
- **Not documenting**: Verbal agreements = future problems
- **No vesting**: Without vesting, an advisor can leave and keep all the equity
