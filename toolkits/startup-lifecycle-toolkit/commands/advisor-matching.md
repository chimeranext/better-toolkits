# /startup-lifecycle:advisor-matching

Guide to finding and evaluating advisors using a 4-axis framework + FAST Agreement.

## Audience

Founders who need advisors for their startup but don't know how to find or compensate them.

## 4-Axis Framework

| Axis | Key questions | Weight |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Domain expertise** | Do they have experience in your industry? | 30% |
| **Network** | Can they open doors? (customers, investors, partners) | 30% |
| **Availability** | Do they have time to dedicate? | 20% |
| **Alignment** | Do they believe in your vision? | 20% |

## Advisor compensation

| Type | Typical equity | Vesting |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Advisor (part-time)** | 0.1-0.5% | 2 years |
| **Advisor (full-time)** | 0.5-1% | 2 years |
| **Board member** | 1-2% | 4 years |

## FAST Agreement (Founder Advisor Standard Template)

- **What it is**: Standardized contract for advisors who receive equity instead of cash
- **Where to get it**: [Google Drive EN](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18UuvJkIydK_mc9vdh_zOnKKQyRZXyLrO/view) | [Google Drive ES](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ub_6rrm95lbMDVsoEv6B-5FCd4QeZ5dO/view)
- **India**: [FAST Agreement Notes](https://mic.gov.in/investor-network/assets/images/resourceKit/FAST-Agreement-Notes.pdf)

## Common mistakes

- **Giving too much equity**: Many founders give too much to advisors and don't get value in return
- **Not documenting**: Verbal agreements = future problems
- **No vesting**: Without vesting, an advisor can leave and keep all the equity
- **No KPIs**: Without clear KPIs, you don't know if the advisor is adding value
