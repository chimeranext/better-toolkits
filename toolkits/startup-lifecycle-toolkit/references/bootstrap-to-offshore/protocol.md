# Protocol: bootstrap-to-offshore

Complete guide to the journey from no money to incorporating a US entity and raising capital.

## Audience

Founders with no experience in the US ecosystem. Typically LATAM founders who:
- Don't have enough savings for US incorporation costs
- Don't know what entity to form or when
- Have an active local SRL/SA or are starting from scratch

## Phase 1 — Save

### US incorporation costs

| Item | Costa Rica | US (Delaware/Wyoming) |
|------|-----------|------------------------|
| Formation | ₡150,000 (~$300) | $500-$2,000 |
| Annual maintenance | ~₡50,000 | $400-$1,500 |
| Banking | Easy | Mercury/Relay (harder) |
| VC fundraising | Impossible | Standard |

### Savings strategies

| Strategy | Description | Estimated time |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Employee** | Save 6-12 months of expenses | 6-12 months |
| **Freelancer** | Invoice and save for incorporation costs | 3-6 months |
| **Active SRL** | Bill services and save for US LLC/PBC | 6-12 months |

## Phase 2 — Choose when to incorporate

| Timing | What to do | Why |
|--------|------------|-----|
| Pre-product, no capital | Do NOT incorporate yet | Save for legal costs |
| Pre-seed, with savings | Delaware LLC (cheap) | Ready for YC/angels |
| Seed/Series A | Convert to Delaware PBC | Institutional VCs + Impact |
| Post-VC, scaling | B Corp certification | Differentiation + Impact VCs |

## Special case: Bootstrap from active LATAM SRL

If you have an SRL in Costa Rica (or similar) billing services:

1. **Keep the SRL active** — bill services from CR
2. **Save revenue** — accumulate for US incorporation costs
3. **When you have $5,000-$10,000 saved** — incorporate LLC/PBC in Delaware
4. **Convert SRL to wholly owned subsidiary** — see `references/subsidiary-conversion/protocol.md`
5. **Bill between SRL and LLC** — establish MSA B2B + transfer pricing
