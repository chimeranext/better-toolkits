# Protocol: vesting

Guide to vesting: schedules, cliff, acceleration, sweat equity.

## Standard Vesting Schedule

### 4 years + 1 year cliff (YC standard)

| Milestone | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Year 1 (cliff)** | 25% of shares vest. If you leave before, you get nothing |
| **Year 2-4** | 1/36 of remaining shares vest monthly |
| **Year 4** | 100% vested |

## Types of Vesting

| Type | Description | When to use it |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Time-based** | Equity earned over time of service | Standard for startups |
| **Milestone-based** | Equity earned upon reaching specific milestones | Less common, for sales roles |
| **Hybrid** | Combination of time + milestone | Specific cases |

## Acceleration

### Single-Trigger Acceleration

- **What**: All unvested equity vests at once upon a single event (almost always an acquisition)
- **Disadvantage**: Acquirers dislike it. They want to retain key members post-acquisition.

### Double-Trigger Acceleration

- **What**: Vesting accelerates only if TWO events occur: (1) acquisition AND (2) involuntary termination
- **Advantage**: Acquirers prefer it because it preserves the requirement of ongoing service to vest.

## Sweat Equity

| Role | Typical equity | Vesting |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Founders** | 20-50% each | 4 years + 1 year cliff |
| **Advisors** | 0.1-1% | 2 years |
| **Early employees** | 0.5-5% | 4 years + 1 year cliff |
| **Contractors** | Varies | By milestone or time |

- Created by Founder Institute
- For advisors who provide connections, credibility, and advice
