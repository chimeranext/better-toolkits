# Protocol: investor-matching

Guide to finding and evaluating investors using a 5-axis framework.

## 5-Axis Framework

| Axis | Key questions | Weight |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Stage fit** | Do they invest in your stage? (pre-seed, seed, Series A) | 25% |
| **Check size** | Does their check size match your raise? | 20% |
| **Thesis alignment** | Does their thesis match your industry/vertical? | 25% |
| **Geography** | Do they invest in your region? | 15% |
| **Value-add** | What do they bring besides capital? (network, expertise, brand) | 15% |

## Channels

| Channel | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **YC Venture Network** | YC investor network |
| **AngelList** | Startup and investor platform |
| **Crunchbase** | Investor database |
| **LinkedIn** | Search VCs by industry |
| **Warm intros** | The best way — ask for introductions |
| **Bridge for Billions** | Mentorship + investor network |

## Process

1. **Define your raise** — amount, stage, investor type
2. **Create investor list** — Crunchbase, AngelList, YC Venture Network (goal: 50-100)
3. **Evaluate with 5-axis framework** — score each investor
4. **Outreach** — warm intro > cold email > cold DM
5. **Pitch meeting** — 10-slide deck, 3-minute pitch, financial model
