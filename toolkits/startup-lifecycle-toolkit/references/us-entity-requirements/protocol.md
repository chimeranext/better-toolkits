# Protocol: us-entity-requirements

Complete requirements for US entity formation and maintenance.

## Formation requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **State selection** | Where partners live, operations, or foreign residents (WY, DE, NM) |
| **Company name** | Must be unique in the state |
| **Registered agent** | Physical address in state, mandatory |
| **Virtual address** | For foreigners, nomads, freelancers ($10-$50/month) |
| **Operating Agreement** | LLC rules, member responsibilities |
| **Bylaws** | C-Corp governance rules |

## EIN requirements

| Scenario | Timeline | Method |
|----------|----------|--------|
| With SSN | Immediate-1 day | IRS online |
| With ITIN | ~45 business days | IRS mail/fax |
| No SSN/ITIN | Variable | Apply for ITIN (W-7) first |

## BOI Report (FinCEN)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **When** | Within 90 days of incorporation |
| **Cost** | Free |
| **Company info** | Name, address, jurisdiction, EIN |
| **Beneficial owners** | Name, address, DOB, ID (25%+ ownership) |
| **Company applicants** | Name, address, DOB, ID |
| **Updates** | Within 30 days of changes |
| **Penalties** | Up to $10,000 + 2 years prison |

## Annual reporting

| Report | Frequency | Where |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Annual report | Annual | State Secretary of State |
| Franchise tax | Annual | State tax authority |
| Federal tax return | Annual | IRS |
| State tax return | Annual | State tax authority |

## Non-resident tax implications

| Scenario | US tax |
|----------|--------|
| Non-resident, no US operations | May be non-taxable |
| Non-resident, US operations | US-sourced income taxable |
| Tax treaty country | May reduce withholding |
