# Protocol: entity-formation

Guide to forming a US entity (Delaware PBC, Wyoming LLC, etc.)

## What entity to form?

| Entity | When to use it | Cost |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Delaware PBC** | B Corp + Impact VCs + social mission | $500-$2,000 |
| **Delaware LLC** | Something simple, convert later | $500-$1,500 |
| **Wyoming LLC** | Privacy + low costs | $500-$1,000 |

## State selection criteria

| Criteria | Best state |
|----------|------------|
| Most partners live there | That state |
| Physical operations (office, warehouse) | That state |
| Most commercial activity | That state |
| Foreign residents (no physical presence) | Wyoming, Delaware, New Mexico |

### State comparison

| State | Franchise tax | Privacy | Best for |
|-------|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Delaware** | ~$300/year | Moderate | VC-backed startups, PBCs |
| **Wyoming** | ~$50/year | High | Privacy-focused, small LLCs |
| **Nevada** | ~$350/year | High | Tax optimization |
| **New Mexico** | $0 | High | Foreign residents, no state income tax |
| **Florida** | $0 (LLC) | Moderate | Latin American founders |

### Step 2 — EIN timeline

| Scenario | Timeline |
|----------|----------|
| With SSN | Immediate to 1 business day |
| Without SSN (ITIN) | ~45 business days |
| Without ITIN | Apply for ITIN first (W-7) |

### Step 3 — Bank account

| Bank | Remote? |
|------|---------|
| Mercury | Yes |
| Relay | Yes |
| Wise Business | Yes |

### Step 4 — BOI Report (REQUIRED)

- **What**: Beneficial Ownership Information report to FinCEN
- **When**: Within 90 days of incorporation
- **Cost**: Free
- **Penalties**: Up to $10,000 and/or 2 years imprisonment

### Step 5 — Registered Agent

- **Required**: In all US states
- **Commercial**: $100-$300/year
- **Self**: Only with physical address in state

### Step 6 — Virtual address

- **For**: Foreigners, digital nomads, freelancers
- **Providers**: Earth Class Mail, Anytime Mailbox, iPostal1
- **Cost**: $10-$50/month

## Essential legal documents

- **Operating Agreement** (LLC) / **Bylaws** (C-Corp): Defines structure, rules, and decision-making
- **FSPA**: Stock purchase agreement between founders
- **IP Assignment**: Assigns founder IP to the company
- **Vesting Schedule**: 4 years + 1 year cliff

## Tax obligations for non-residents

| Scenario | Tax implication |
|----------|-----------------|
| Non-resident, no US operations | Income may be non-taxable |
| Non-resident, US operations | Subject to US taxes on US-sourced income |
| Tax treaty country | May reduce withholding |

## Reporting obligations

| Report | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| Annual report | Annual |
| Franchise tax | Annual |
| BOI Report | One-time (update on changes) |
| Federal tax return | Annual |
| State tax return | Annual |
