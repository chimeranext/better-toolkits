# Protocol: cofounder-matching

Guide to finding and evaluating co-founders using a 6-axis framework.

## 6-Axis Framework

| Axis | Key questions | Weight |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Domain fit** | Do they understand the domain/problem? | 20% |
| **Skill complementarity** | Do their skills complement yours? | 25% |
| **Values alignment** | Do they share values and vision? | 20% |
| **Equity expectations** | Do they agree on the split? | 15% |
| **Time commitment** | Can they dedicate full-time? | 10% |
| **Track record** | Do they have relevant experience? | 10% |

## Process

1. **Define your profile** — role, missing skills, equity expectation, commitment
2. **Find candidates** — YC Co-Founder Matching, Founder Institute, LinkedIn, events
3. **Evaluate candidates** — 6-axis score
4. **Equity conversation** — 50/50 default, vesting 4 years + 1 year cliff
5. **FAST Agreement** — for advisors (0.1-1% equity, 2 years vesting)
