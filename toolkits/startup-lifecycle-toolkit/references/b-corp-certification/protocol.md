# Protocol: b-corp-certification

Guide to obtaining B Corp certification (B Corporation).

## B Corp vs PBC

| | **PBC (Legal)** | **B Corp (Certification)** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Legal entity type | Private certification from B Lab |
| **Where** | Delaware, Wyoming, +30 states | Global (10,000+ companies) |
| **Requirement** | Articles of incorporation + annual report | B Lab Standards audit + score ≥ 80 |
| **Relationship** | A PBC **can** be B Corp certified | A B Corp **must** be PBC or equivalent legal |

## Certification path

### Step 1 — Verify eligibility

- Be a for-profit company
- Have at least 12 months of operations (or less if you meet certain requirements)
- Have a legal structure that considers stakeholder governance (PBC or equivalent)

### Step 2 — B Impact Assessment (BIA)

- Free online assessment at bimpactassessment.net
- Measures impact in 5 areas: Governance, Workers, Community, Environment, Customers
- Minimum score: 80 out of 200 points

### Step 3 — B Lab review

- B Lab reviews your BIA + supporting documents
- They may request additional evidence
- Typically takes 3-6 months

### Step 4 — Certification

- Pay annual fee (based on revenue, typically $2,000-$50,000)
- Sign the B Corp Declaration of Interdependence
- Use the B Corp logo

### Step 5 — Maintain certification

- Recertification every 3 years
- Maintain score ≥ 80
- Comply with stakeholder governance legal requirements
