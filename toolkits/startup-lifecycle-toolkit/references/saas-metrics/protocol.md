# Protocol: saas-metrics

Guía de SaaS metrics para startups.

## Métricas clave

| Métrica | Fórmula | Benchmark |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue | — |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue = MRR × 12 | — |
| **Churn rate** | (Clientes perdidos / Clientes al inicio) × 100 | < 5% mensual |
| **LTV** | (ARPA × Gross margin) / Churn rate | — |
| **CAC** | Costo de adquisición de clientes | — |
| **LTV:CAC** | LTV / CAC | > 3:1 |
| **Payback period** | CAC / (ARPA × Gross margin) | < 12 meses |
| **Burn multiple** | (Net cash burn) / (Net new ARR) | < 1.5x |
| **Rule of 40** | (Revenue growth %) + (Profit margin %) | > 40% |

## Growth metrics (PostHog)

| Métrica | Descripción |
|---------|-------------|
| **ICP retention** | Retención filtrada por Ideal Customer Profile |
| **Average quarterly MRR** | Promedio de 3 meses de MRR, annualizado |
| **Noise sources** | Fuentes de ruido documentadas y ajustadas |

## Métricas por etapa

| Etapa | Métrica clave |
|-------|---------------|
| **Pre-product** | Customer interviews, problem validation |
| **MVP** | Activation rate, time to value |
| **Product-Market Fit** | Retention, NPS, organic growth |
| **Growth** | MRR growth, CAC payback, LTV:CAC |
| **Scale** | Net revenue retention, burn multiple, Rule of 40 |
