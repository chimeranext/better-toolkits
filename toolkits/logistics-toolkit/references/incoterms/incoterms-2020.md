# INCOTERMS 2020

## The 11 codes

| Group | Code | Name | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E | EXW | Ex Works | Seller places goods at disposal |
| F | FCA | Free Carrier | Seller delivers to carrier |
| F | FAS | Free Alongside Ship | Seller alongside vessel |
| F | FOB | Free On Board | Seller loads on board vessel |
| C | CFR | Cost and Freight | Seller pays freight to destination port |
| C | CIF | Cost, Insurance and Freight | Seller pays freight + insurance |
| C | CPT | Carriage Paid To | Seller pays carriage to destination |
| C | CIP | Carriage and Insurance Paid To | Seller pays carriage + insurance |
| D | DAP | Delivered at Place | Seller delivers at named place |
| D | DPU | Delivered at Place Unloaded | Seller delivers + unloads |
| D | DDP | Delivered Duty Paid | Seller delivers, duty paid |

## FOB vs CIF

- **FOB**: seller delivers on board vessel at origin port. Buyer arranges main carriage + insurance.
- **CIF**: seller pays freight + insurance to destination port. Risk transfers at origin, but cost/insurance covered to destination.

## Glossary

- FOB = value at origin port.
- CIF = FOB + freight + insurance + import taxes.
- FCA = applies to air freight (seller delivers to carrier at named place).

## Guidance

- **FCA** for air freight from China (e.g. sensors from NYC).
- **FOB** for sea freight.
- Choose term that matches transport mode and who covers insurance.
