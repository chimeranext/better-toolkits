# Territorio Aduanero

- **Zona primaria**: area where customs operations physically occur — ports, land borders,
  airports, and customs warehouses. Merchandise here is under customs control.
- **Zona secundaria**: free-circulation area; once cleared, goods circulate freely.

Merchandise entering the territorio aduanero must be assigned a *régimen aduanero* (see
`regimes.md`). The **DUA** is the controlling declaration.
