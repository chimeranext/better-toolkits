# Costa Rica Customs Regimes

## Legal framework

- **CAUCA** (Ley 8360): Central American Uniform Customs Code. A *régimen aduanero* is the
  destination of merchandise under customs control.
- **Ley General de Aduanas** (7575): defines the *territorio aduanero*.
- **Territorio aduanero**:
  - **Zona primaria** — where customs operations occur (ports, borders, airports, warehouses).
  - **Zona secundaria** — the free-circulation area.

## Regimes

| Régimen | Uso | Marco |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Importación definitiva | Goods enter free circulation | CAUCA / 7575 |
| Importación temporal | Temporary admission (e.g. samples, re-export) | CAUCA |
| Tránsito | Movement between customs points / countries | CAUCA |
| Depósito aduanero | Storage under customs control | CAUCA |
| Zonas francas | In-bond manufacturing/trade zone | Ley 7210 |
| Recinto aduanero | Private customs facility | CAUCA |

## Almacén fiscal

- Storage under customs control.
- **DUA** confección (declaración única aduanera) is the customs declarant's responsibility.
- **Agencia aduanal** carries declarant liability.
