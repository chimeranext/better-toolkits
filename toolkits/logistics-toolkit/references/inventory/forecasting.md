# Demand Forecasting & Production Planning

## Demand forecasting

- **Qualitative**: expert judgment, Delphi, market surveys.
- **Quantitative**: time-series (moving average, exponential smoothing) and regression.

## Production planning

- **MPS (Master Production Scheduling)**: what to produce, when, how much.
- **Production planning**: plant-level scheduling + capacity planning.
