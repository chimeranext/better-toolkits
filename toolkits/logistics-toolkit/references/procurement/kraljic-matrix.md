# Kraljic Portfolio Matrix

Categorizes purchases by **supply risk** (x-axis) vs **profit impact** (y-axis). The strategy
depends on which quadrant a purchase falls into.

| Quadrant | Supply risk | Profit impact | Sourcing strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategic** | High | High | Partnership, long-term contracts, joint development |
| **Leverage** | Low | High | Competitive bidding, supplier leverage, global sourcing |
| **Bottleneck** | High | Low | Secure supply, dual sourcing, buffer stock |
| **Non-critical** | Low | Low | Efficient processing, standardized, minimize admin |

## Use with P2P

- Strategic/bottleneck → more rigorous quotation and approval (steps 3–6).
- Leverage/non-critical → streamlined, catalog-based purchasing.
