# Procure-to-Pay (P2P) — 10 steps

1. **Requisition** — need identified and logged by the requester.
2. **Requisition Approval** — budget holder approves the request.
3. **Quotation** — send RFP / obtain bids; use Kraljic to choose the process depth.
4. **Quotation Approval** — select supplier; agree price/terms.
5. **Purchase Order (PO)** — formal commitment; must reference INCOTERM, delivery, payment terms.
6. **PO Approval** — procurement + finance sign-off.
7. **Goods Receipt** — receive and inspect goods vs PO.
8. **Supplier Performance** — record quality / on-time delivery (feeds SRM).
9. **Invoice Approval** — three-way match (PO ↔ receipt ↔ invoice).
10. **Vendor Payment** — pay per agreed terms.

## SRM (Supplier Relationship Management)

- Onboarding (KYC, tax, banking, compliance).
- Performance evaluation (quality, delivery, cost, responsiveness).
- Category strategy driven by the Kraljic matrix (see `kraljic-matrix.md`).
