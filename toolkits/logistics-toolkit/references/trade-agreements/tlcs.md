## Certificado de origen

- Required to claim **tariff preference** under a TLC.
- Usually auto-generated (e.g. in J06) from the commercial data.
- Keep per-TLC format and validity; see `certificado-origen.md`.

## Guidance

- Confirm the TLC applies to the goods' **origin** and the buyer's country.
- For non-preference shipments, the certificado de origen may be unnecessary.
