# Protocol: study-in-germany (digital-nomad)

Operating (or keeping passive) a US Single-Member LLC while studying in
Germany under §16b AufenthG.

## Student visa facts

- **Self-employment prohibited by default** under §16b; permitted exception:
  §21 Abs. 6 AufenthG (discretionary LEA authorization, rarely granted to
  undergraduates).
- **Dependent employment** allowed: 140 full / 280 half days per year, or
  ≤ 20 h/week during term. Werkstudent contracts are the standard legal path.
- **Territorialität**: services performed physically in Germany are German
  activity regardless of the Wyoming invoice, Stripe, or Mercury flow.

## Tax exposure

- > 183 days/year → German tax residence → worldwide income (*Welteinkommen*).
- **§10 AO**: place of effective management (*Ort der Geschäftsleitung*) in
  Germany → LLC can be re-qualified → Körperschaftsteuer + Gewerbesteuer +
  penalty risk (*Steuerhinterziehung*).
- **Scheinselbstständigkeit**: integrated client work → Deutsche
  Rentenversicherung retroactive contributions from the client.

## Recommended route

Student visa first → keep LLC **passive** (maintenance only) → switch to a
work/freelance title after graduation (or §21 Abs. 5 Freiberufler instead of
the student route).

## §21 Abs. 6 application package

- Scope-of-Services letter (services outside Germany or hard ≤ 15–20 h/week
  cap), business plan with ECTS split, time-tracking, contracts predating
  arrival do not exempt.

## Financing

- **Sperrkonto** ≈ €11,904/year (≈ €992/month released) + tuition payments;
  student jobs ≈ €14–20/h in tech.
