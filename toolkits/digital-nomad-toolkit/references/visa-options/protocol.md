# Protocol: visa-options (digital-nomad)

Guide to visa options for digital nomads who want to work from the US.

## US visa options

| Visa | Best for | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| B-1 | Business visits | Up to 6 months |
| E-2 | Treaty country investors | Up to 5 years |
| H-1B | Specialty occupations | Up to 6 years |
| O-1 | Extraordinary ability | Up to 3 years |
| ESTA | Short business visits | Up to 90 days |

## E-2 Treaty Investor Visa

- Treaty country nationality required
- Substantial investment (typically $100K+)
- Must be actively operating
- Must own 50%+ of the business

**Treaty countries**: Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico, Spain, and many others.

## Digital nomad visas (outside US)

| Country | Visa | Income requirement |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Portugal | D7 | Passive income |
| Spain | Digital Nomad Visa | Remote work |
| Costa Rica | Digital Nomad Visa | $3,000/month |
