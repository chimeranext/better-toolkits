# Protocol: route-decision (digital-nomad)

Routing across nine study/founder countries for US Single-Member LLC
operators.

## Study matrix (condensed)

| Country | Student work | Active LLC | Exit |
|---------|--------------|------------|------|
| 🇩🇪 Germany | ≤20 h/week dependent; self-employment banned (§16b) | ❌ | Work/freelance title (or Freiberufler §21 Abs. 5) |
| 🇨🇦 Canada | On-campus only ~20 h/week; no work permit | ❌ | PGWP (open work) |
| 🇮🇪 Ireland | Stamp 2: 20 h term / 40 h vacation; no business or trade | ❌ | Stamp 1G (no self-employment) → permit |
| 🇦🇺 Australia | 48 h/fortnight in session; self-employment allowed | ⚠️ Inside the cap | 485 (2–3 yrs, unrestricted) |
| 🇯🇵 Japan | 28 h/week part-time; no business management | ❌ | Startup Visa → Business Manager (¥30M+) |
| 🇸🇬 Singapore | ≤16 h/week term (listed schools); no business | ❌ | EntrePass / ONE Pass (AI and Tech) — SG Pte Ltd |
| 🇰🇷 South Korea | D-2 permit ~20 h/week standard (tiered 10–35 h) | ❌ | D-10-2 → D-8-4 (OASIS) → F-2/F-5 |
| 🇨🇳 China | Endorsed work-study 8 h/week, 40 h/month (school + PSB) | ❌ | WFOE + work permit A/B → Z visa; K visa (STEM, no employer) |
| 🇹🇼 Taiwan | ARC + WDA permit 20 h/week term, unlimited breaks | ❌ | Digital Nomad Visa (own LLC remotely) / Gold Card / Entrepreneur Visa |

## Founder anchors

- 🇯🇵 KK/GK with ¥30M capital + JP-resident FTE + real office.
- 🇸🇬 ACRA Pte Ltd ≥ 30% yours (EntrePass) or personal track (ONE Pass).
- 🇰🇷 Korean entity + OASIS points (not capital).
- 🇩🇪 Freiberufler or GmbH.
- 🇮🇪 employment permit or STEP (€50k).
- 🇨🇦🇦🇺 no dedicated startup visa — use PGWP/485.
- 🇨🇳 WFOE subsidiary of the US LLC + work permit A/B (Shenzhen/Qianhai);
  K visa as a sponsor-free STEM entry.
- 🇹🇼 no entity needed for the Digital Nomad Visa / Gold Card; local
  company only for the Entrepreneur Visa traction path.

A Wyoming LLC cannot sponsor any of these — plan a local entity for the
founder phase; keep the LLC passive until then. Taiwan's Digital Nomad Visa
is the one route built to run the LLC itself remotely.

## Selection logic

1. Need active LLC income while there? → only Australia tolerates it, capped.
2. Study or business priority? → study: passive LLC + founder exit; business:
   founder visa first.
3. Speed of unrestricted work post-grad: TW Digital Nomad Visa (own LLC
   remotely) > AU 485 ≈ CA PGWP > IE 1G > KR pipeline > JP/CN founder bars.
4. Capital: ¥30M+ → JP direct path; S$100k round/accelerator → SG; WFOE
   runway → CN; low → KR OASIS, TW Entrepreneur Visa, or study-then-485/PGWP.
5. Tax tolerance: all nine → worldwide income + effective-management/CFC
   scrutiny; passive LLC + clean US filings is the zero-exposure posture.
