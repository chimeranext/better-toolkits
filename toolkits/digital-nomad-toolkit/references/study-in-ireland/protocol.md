# Protocol: study-in-ireland (digital-nomad)

Keeping a US Single-Member LLC alive while studying in Ireland on Stamp 2
student permission.

## Student permission facts

- **Stamp 2** (ILEP course): casual employment **20 h/week** in term,
  **40 h/week** in the fixed vacations (June–September, 15 December –
  15 January). The cap is a total across employers.
- Explicit condition: **"You must not engage in any other business or
  trade"** — active LLC operation is out. Self-employment is not allowed even
  in internships/placements.
- **Stamp 2A** (non-ILEP course): **no work at all**.
- Permission to work without an employment permit exists only for Stamp 1G,
  Stamp 4, Stamp 5.

## Business-permission routes

- **Stamp 1 + ISD letter** (must name business/trade permission) — usually
  via Critical Skills or General Employment Permit.
- **Start-Up Entrepreneur Programme (STEP)**: innovative business, ≥ €50,000
  funding; not retail/catering/personal services.

## Post-graduation

- **Third Level Graduate Programme (Stamp 1G)**: 12 months (Level 8) or
  **24 months** (Level 9+; renewed at 12 months on job-seeking evidence);
  apply within 6 months of the award; work without a permit — but
  **self-employment is not allowed**. Overall student-permission caps: 7 years
  (L8) / 8 years (L9+).

## Tax exposure

- 183+ days → Irish residence → worldwide income; effective management of the
  LLC from Ireland draws Revenue scrutiny (§10-AO-style).
