# Protocol: study-in-japan (digital-nomad)

The student and founder faces of Japan for US Single-Member LLC operators.

## Student visa (留学)

- Part-time work via **Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than
  Permitted** (資格外活動許可): **28 h/week** (8 h/day long vacations); no
  adult entertainment. Employment only — **managing a company is not
  covered**; a side-business permission for managers is not granted in
  practice.
- LLC posture: fully passive.

## Founder routes

- **Startup Visa** (Designated Activities, ≤ 2 years, nationwide since
  Jan 2025): Business Preparation Activity Plan → certified Foreign
  Entrepreneurship Promotion Organization → Certificate of Confirmation →
  grace period to meet Business Manager requirements.
- **Business Manager (経営・管理)** — all required since the Oct 2025
  tightening: capital **≥ ¥30M** (source-of-funds scrutiny; student
  earnings rejected as source), ≥ 1 full-time employee (JP national/PR/
  spouse-type, 30+ h/week, direct contract, social insurance), real office
  (**virtual/shared offices rejected**), business plan verified by a
  professional, ≥ 3 years management experience or master's+, Japanese
  proficiency. No side jobs on the visa itself.
- **J-Find** (Designated Activities — Future Creation Talent): graduates of
  top-100 (≥ 2 rankings) universities within 5 years, ≈ ¥200k savings;
  ≤ 2 years job-hunting/startup prep with unrestricted part-time work.

## Tax exposure

- Foreign company with **effective management** in Japan → treated as a
  Japanese corporation for tax; worldwide income for residents.
