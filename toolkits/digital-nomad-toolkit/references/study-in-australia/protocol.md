# Protocol: study-in-australia (digital-nomad)

Operating a US Single-Member LLC within an Australian student visa
(subclass 500).

## Student visa facts

- **48 hours per fortnight** while the course is in session (conditions
  8104/8105; fortnight = 14 days starting Monday, all work combined);
  **unlimited** during scheduled breaks; no work before the course starts;
  research master's/doctorate exempt once commenced.
- **Self-employment is allowed** — freelancing, ABN, directorship count as
  "work"; an ABN grants no extra hours. Passive ownership is not work.
- All active hours (clients, admin, marketing, invoicing) count toward the
  cap — keep timesheets.

## Risk beyond hours

- Genuine Student requirement: if Home Affairs concludes you are in Australia
  primarily to run a business, the visa and future applications are at risk.

## Tax exposure

- Residency tests → worldwide income; ABN is a tax identifier only; GST may
  apply above the turnover threshold.
- A foreign company **centrally managed and controlled** from Australia can
  be treated as an Australian tax resident (effective-management logic).

## Exit: Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

- Post-Higher Education: usually **2–3 years** by qualification (+1–2 in
  regional areas), **unrestricted** work; age ≤ 35; ≥ 2 academic years
  (92 weeks) in ≤ 16 calendar months.

## Financing

- DHA financial-capity evidence at application (living + tuition + travel —
  verify current figure); OSHC health cover mandatory.
