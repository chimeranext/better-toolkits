# Protocol: study-in-taiwan (digital-nomad)

The student and founder faces of Taiwan for US Single-Member LLC operators.

## Student visa (ARC + work permit)

- Degree students receive an **ARC**, then a **part-time work permit** via
  the Workforce Development Agency online system (schools typically
  administer it): **20 h/week** in semester, **unlimited** in summer and
  winter breaks (Employment Services Act exception for international
  students). Fine for working without a permit: **NTD 30k–150k**.
- Non-degree language students are generally not eligible until they have
  studied in Taiwan for a full year — confirm with your school's
  international office.
- Post-graduation internship (bachelor's ≥ 70 average or master's+, via the
  university): up to **1 year**.
- Managing your own company is **not** covered — keep the LLC **passive**
  (`maintain-llc-abroad`).
- Tax: residents taxed on **worldwide income** (> 183 days); minimum-tax
  reporting for foreign-sourced income; **CFC rules since 2023** for
  foreign entities the individual controls.

## Founder routes

- **Digital Nomad Visa** (NDC): multiple-entry, **6 months, extendable to 2
  years total**; visa-exempt nationals (US included), 20+; income proof
  **US$40k** (30+) or **US$20k** (20–29) in either of the last 2 years;
  work for a foreign employer or **your own foreign company** only, not for
  Taiwanese clients; apply overseas or at BOCA in Taiwan before the current
  stay expires.
- **Employment Gold Card** (Foreign Professional Act Art. 8): resident visa
  + open work permit + ARC + re-entry in one; **no employer**, 1–3 years, 12
  professional fields (NSTC science & technology, MoDA digital, etc.);
  counts toward permanent-residence points.
- **Entrepreneur Visa**: MOEA qualification without a company — VC/NDF or
  approved crowdfunding ≥ NTD 2M; incubator/park residency + recommendation
  (e.g. Startup Terrace); notable patents/expertise; competition awards; or
  ≥ NTD 1M invested in an innovative startup as legal rep/manager/director.
  Initial ≤ 2 years; extensions ≤ 2 years each require **NTD 3M revenue or
  NTD 1M opex or ≥ 3 full-time ROC employees** (or equivalent). Gold Card
  conversion available for qualifying holders.
- **Company setup** (subsidiary of the US LLC): name reservation → MOEA
  Department of Investment Review approval → remit capital → capital
  verification → company registration (< NTD 500M paid-in at city/county)
  → tax registration.
- **Tax**: CIT **20% + 5% surtax** on undistributed earnings; non-resident
  dividend WHT **21%** — **no US–Taiwan income tax treaty in force** (1984
  agreement covers shipping/air only; the US double-tax relief bill passed
  the House Jan 2025, still pending in the Senate).
- **Ecosystem**: StartupBlink 2026 — Taiwan #20 globally (top-20 first),
  Hardware & IoT #7; Taipei–Hsinchu–Miaoli a ranked region; Taipei ~$12B
  ecosystem value (Startup Genome); **COMPUTEX / InnoVEX 2027: June 1–4,
  TaiNEX 2** (exhibitors founded ≥ 2019-01-01; early-bird until 2027-01-31).
