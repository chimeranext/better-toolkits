# Protocol: study-in-singapore (digital-nomad)

The student and founder faces of Singapore for US Single-Member LLC
operators.

## Student Pass (ICA)

- Work exemption only for full-time students at **listed institutions**;
  exchange students: no work; private-institution students generally need
  MOM approval.
- Term: **≤ 16 h/week** or curriculum internship/attachment; vacations:
  unlimited (within the exemption list).
- No business/self-employment under the exemption; unauthorized work is a
  criminal offence (revocation, fines, entry ban).

## Founder passes (MOM / EDB) — anchored to a Singapore Pte Ltd

- **EntrePass**: ACRA Pte Ltd with **≥ 30%** yours, venture-backed or
  innovative tech, plus ≥ 1 criterion: **S$100,000** single-round funding ·
  government-recognised / renowned incubator-accelerator (e.g., YC) ·
  founded-and-sold tech business · registered IP · IHL/A*STAR/CREATE
  research collaboration. Not eligible: F&B, massage/TCM, employment
  agencies, geomancy. Validity 1→1→2 years; renewal tracks local hiring +
  business spending; companies > 12 months assessed on renewal criteria.
- **Tech.Pass**: last drawn fixed monthly salary **≥ S$22,500** + ≥ 5
  cumulative years in a leading role at a tech company ≥ US$500M valuation
  or ≥ US$30M funding; 2 + 2 years; multiple concurrent roles.
- **Sunset**: from **28 January 2027** no new/renewal Tech.Pass — replaced by
  **ONE Pass (AI and Tech)**; EDB Global Founder Programme is the current
  founder umbrella.

## Tax note

- Headline **17%**, territorial framing; a company managed and controlled
  from Singapore can be pulled into net; US filings unchanged.
