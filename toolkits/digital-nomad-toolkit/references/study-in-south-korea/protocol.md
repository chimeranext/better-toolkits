# Protocol: study-in-south-korea (digital-nomad)

The student and founder faces of South Korea for US Single-Member LLC
operators.

## Student visa (D-2)

- Any paid work needs a prior **part-time work permit** (시간제취업 허가;
  HiKorea online-only since Aug 2025). One hour without it = illegal
  employment.
- Eligibility: GPA ≥ 2.0 previous semester + TOPIK 3–4 / KIIP thresholds
  tiered by degree level.
- Hours: standard **≤ 20 h/week Mon–Fri** in semester, weekends/vacations
  unrestricted; university tiered tables run ≈10 h/week (no TOPIK) to
  30–35 h/week (TOPIK 4, graduate).
- Rules: max 2 workplaces (D-2); simple part-time work only (no
  manufacturing/construction without TOPIK 4, no dispatch/subcontract, no
  long commutes); new permit per employer; validity ≤ 1 year.
- **Operating your own business is not covered** — that is D-8/D-9 status.

## Founder pipeline (OASIS — Global Startup Immigration Center)

| Stage | Visa | Bar |
|-------|------|-----|
| Prepare | **D-10-2** | OASIS orientation (Level 4+); 6-month extensions ≤ 2 years |
| Launch | **D-8-4** | Degree (associate+ in KR / bachelor's+ abroad) + OASIS **≥ 60/300** points + IP/tech; Korean entity + registration |
| Settle | **F-2** | Points + D-8 tenure (KIIP helps) |
| Permanent | **F-5** | 3 years on D-8 + ≥ KRW 300M invested + ≥ 2 Korean employees; D-8-4 may convert directly when met |

K-Startup Grand Challenge adds recognition/points.

## Tax exposure

- Residents taxed on worldwide income; **K-CFC rules** can attribute income
  of foreign entities controlled by Korean residents — active management of
  a Wyoming LLC from Seoul is in scope.
