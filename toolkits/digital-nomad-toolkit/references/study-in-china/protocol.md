# Protocol: study-in-china (digital-nomad)

The student and founder faces of mainland China for US Single-Member LLC
operators.

## Student visa (X1/X2)

- **X1** (study > 180 days) = study-type residence permit; **X2** (≤ 180 days)
  = stay permit. A **work permit (Z) is mutually exclusive** with study
  status.
- Off-campus work-study requires a **residence-permit endorsement**: school
  approval → PSB exit-entry endorsement of employer, location and duration
  (Entry-Exit Regulations Art. 22). Caps per MOE work-study measures:
  **8 h/week, 40 h/month** in term; **16 h/week, 80 h/month** in vacations.
  Conditions: 18+, valid study permit with ≥ 6 months remaining, good
  standing, insurance, employer registered with the local exit-entry bureau.
- **Internship endorsement**: same school + PSB route, must relate to the
  field of study. X2 stay permits **cannot** carry internship endorsements —
  only study residence permits and private-affairs visas (Shenzhen PSB).
- Managing your own company is **not** covered by the endorsement:
  unauthorized work → fines per day, detention, deportation. LLC stays
  **passive** (`maintain-llc-abroad`).
- Tax: effective-management rule can make a foreign company a **Chinese tax
  resident** on worldwide income (same logic as Germany's §10 AO / Japan).

## Founder routes

- **WFOE subsidiary of the US LLC** — standard under FIL 2020 + Company Law
  2023. Shenzhen/Qianhai path: negative-list check (FTZ list shorter) → AMR
  registration + business license + initial foreign-investment information
  report → CNY basic account + FX account → SAFE registration → tax
  registration. Subscribed capital **paid within 5 years** (existing FIEs
  until 30 Jun 2027). US documents need an **apostille** (China in the
  Apostille Convention since 7 Nov 2023).
- **Work permit A/B → Z visa → work residence permit**: A = high-end talent
  incl. international entrepreneurs (Shenzhen: salary ≥ 6× local average,
  ≈ ¥55k/month); B = degree + 2 yrs experience, < 60 years (salary ≥ 4×,
  ≈ ¥37k/month). Apply via STIC → certificate → Z visa → residence permit
  within 30 days of entry; high tiers up to 5 years.
- **K visa** (since 1 Oct 2025, Decree 814): young STEM talent
  (bachelor's+ from well-known institutions or STEM faculty/researchers), no
  employer/inviter required, generous entries/validity/stay, business and
  entrepreneurship activities allowed after entry.
- **Tax**: no US-China treaty for disregarded LLCs; 10% dividend WHT needs
  corporate beneficial-owner status → check-the-box election → GILTI/Subpart
  F + Form 5471 on the US side. Qianhai: 15% CIT for encouraged industries.
- **Ecosystem**: Shenzhen hardware/robotics/AI cluster (~$1T ecosystem value,
  Tencent, BYD, 49 unicorns); #1 R&D-engine ranking (Startup Genome GSER).
