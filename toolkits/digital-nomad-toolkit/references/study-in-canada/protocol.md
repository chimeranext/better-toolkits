# Protocol: study-in-canada (digital-nomad)

Keeping a US Single-Member LLC alive while studying in Canada on a study
permit.

## Student permit facts

- **A study permit is not a work permit.** Off-campus work and
  self-employment are not authorized by default.
- Allowed: **on-campus only**, ~20 h/week while classes are in session
  (full-time in scheduled breaks). IRCC adjusts these rules often — verify
  current policy.
- Invoicing US clients from Canadian soil is **work performed in Canada** —
  the Wyoming entity and payment rails do not change that.

## Risk

- Unauthorized work can jeopardize the study permit, PGWP applications, and
  permanent-residence pathways.

## Tax exposure

- Residency tests → tax resident (worldwide income); students commonly meet
  them.
- Canada–US treaty + foreign tax credit (Form T2209) prevent most double
  taxation; **Form T1135** if specified foreign property cost exceeds
  CAD 100,000.

## Recommended route

Study permit → LLC **passive** (maintenance only) → **PGWP** (open work,
program-dependent) → resume active work; or move on via visa-options.

## Legal income during the semester

- On-campus roles (TA, research assistant, campus jobs) only.
