# Protocol: entity-formation (digital-nomad)

Guide to forming a US Single-Member LLC for freelancers and digital nomads.

## What entity to form?

| Entity | Best for | Cost |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Single-Member LLC** | Freelancers, digital nomads | $500-$1,500 |
| **Wyoming LLC** | Privacy + low costs | $500-$1,000 |
| **New Mexico LLC** | No state income tax | $500-$1,000 |

## Steps

1. **Choose state**: Wyoming, New Mexico, Florida
2. **Choose service**: Firstbase ($399), Doola ($297)
3. **Get EIN**: With SSN: immediate. Without: ~45 days
4. **Open bank account**: Mercury, Relay, Wise (all remote)
5. **File BOI Report**: Within 90 days, free
6. **Registered Agent**: $100-$300/year
7. **Virtual address**: $10-$50/month

## Essential documents

- Operating Agreement (even for single-member)
- EIN from IRS
- BOI Report to FinCEN
