# Protocol: banking (digital-nomad)

Guide to opening a US bank account remotely as a digital nomad.

## Remote banking options

| Bank | Requirements | Remote? |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Mercury | EIN + Articles of Organization | Yes |
| Relay | EIN + Operating Agreement | Yes |
| Wise Business | EIN + Proof of address | Yes |
| Brex | EIN + Revenue | Yes |

## Requirements

- EIN from IRS
- Articles of Organization from state
- Operating Agreement
- Photo ID (passport)
- Physical address (virtual accepted by some)

## Recommended setup

1. **Primary**: Mercury or Relay
2. **Secondary**: Wise Business (international transfers)
3. **Optional**: Traditional bank
