# Protocol: msa-b2b (digital-nomad)

Guide to billing international services as a US Single-Member LLC.

## How to bill

1. **Invoice**: Your LLC name + address, client details, amount
2. **Receive payment**: Wire, Wise, PayPal, Stripe
3. **Record**: Keep invoices, receipts, accounting records

## MSA B2B elements

- Scope of services
- Pricing and payment terms
- IP ownership
- Confidentiality (NDA)
- Limitation of liability
- Termination and transition

## Tax implications

| Scenario | Tax implication |
|----------|-----------------|
| Client in US | Subject to US taxes |
| Client outside US | May be non-taxable |
| Tax treaty country | May reduce withholding |
