## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Study-abroad expansion: **9 commands + 9 reference protocols**
- `study-in-germany` — §16b self-employment ban, §21 Abs. 6 exception, Territorialitätsprinzip, §10 AO effective-management risk, Sperrkonto, Werkstudent route
- `study-in-canada` — study permit grants no work permit (on-campus only), passive-LLC posture, PGWP exit
- `study-in-ireland` — Stamp 2 hours, "no business or trade" condition, Third Level Graduate 1G exit
- `study-in-australia` — subclass 500 48h/fortnight cap, permitted self-employment, 485 exit
- `study-in-japan` — student visa 28h/week, Startup Visa → Business Manager (¥30M, Oct-2025 tightening), J-Find
- `study-in-singapore` — Student Pass 16h/week, EntrePass criteria, Tech.Pass sunset (ONE Pass AI and Tech from Jan 2027)
- `study-in-south-korea` — D-2 tiered part-time permit, OASIS pipeline D-10-2 → D-8-4 → F-2/F-5, K-CFC note
- `maintain-llc-abroad` — annual checklist, address-change triggers, dormancy, platform hygiene
- `route-decision` — 7-country matrix × posture × graduation exit + founder-anchors table
- Facts verified against official sources (ISA/JETRO, ICA/MOM/EDB, Hi Korea/MOJ, ISD, Home Affairs, IRCC); every command carries a verify-current-rules note
