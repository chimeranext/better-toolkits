# digital-nomad

Guide for freelancers and digital nomads who want to operate as a US Single-Member LLC.

**FREE** — This toolkit is completely free. No entrance fee.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads who:
- Want to stay as a Single-Member LLC (not jump to startup/shared services)
- Need to bill international clients in USD
- Want to keep taxes clean in each country
- Work remotely from anywhere in the world

## Available Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| `/digital-nomad:entity-formation` | Form Single-Member LLC + virtual address + registered agent |
| `/digital-nomad:tax-compliance` | Non-resident taxes + annual reporting + tax treaties |
| `/digital-nomad:banking` | Remote bank account opening (Mercury, Relay, Wise) |
| `/digital-nomad:msa-b2b` | How to bill international services as an LLC |
| `/digital-nomad:visa-options` | Visa options for digital nomads (B-1, E-2, H-1B) |
