# /digital-nomad:entity-formation

Guide to forming a US Single-Member LLC for freelancers and digital nomads.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads who want to operate as a US Single-Member LLC.

## What entity to form?

| Entity | Best for | Cost |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Single-Member LLC** | Freelancers, digital nomads, solo entrepreneurs | $500-$1,500 |
| **Wyoming LLC** | Privacy + low costs | $500-$1,000 |
| **New Mexico LLC** | No state income tax, foreign residents | $500-$1,000 |

## Steps to incorporate

### Step 1 — Choose state

| Criteria | Best state |
|----------|------------|
| No physical presence | Wyoming, New Mexico |
| Privacy | Wyoming, New Mexico |
| Low costs | Wyoming (~$50/year), New Mexico ($0) |
| Proximity to LATAM | Florida |

### Step 2 — Choose formation service

| Service | Cost |
|---------|------|
| Firstbase | $399 |
| Doola | $297 |
| Direct (state SOS) | $300 |

### Step 3 — Get EIN

| Scenario | Timeline |
|----------|----------|
| With SSN | Immediate to 1 business day |
| Without SSN (ITIN) | ~45 business days |
| Without ITIN | Apply for ITIN first (W-7) |

### Step 4 — Open bank account (remote)

| Bank | Requirements | Remote? |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Mercury | EIN + Articles of Organization | Yes |
| Relay | EIN + Operating Agreement | Yes |
| Wise Business | EIN + Proof of address | Yes |

### Step 5 — File BOI Report (REQUIRED)

- **What**: Beneficial Ownership Information report to FinCEN
- **When**: Within 90 days of incorporation
- **Cost**: Free
- **Penalties**: Up to $10,000 and/or 2 years imprisonment

### Step 6 — Registered Agent

- **Required**: In all US states
- **Commercial**: $100-$300/year
- **Self**: Only with physical address in state

### Step 7 — Virtual address

- **For**: Foreigners, digital nomads, freelancers
- **Providers**: Earth Class Mail, Anytime Mailbox, iPostal1
- **Cost**: $10-$50/month

## Essential documents

- **Operating Agreement**: Defines structure, rules, and decision-making (even for single-member)
- **EIN**: From IRS
- **BOI Report**: To FinCEN
