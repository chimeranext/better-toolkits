# /digital-nomad:study-in-south-korea

Two faces of South Korea for LLC operators: the **D-2 student visa**
(tiered part-time permits) and the **OASIS startup pipeline**
(D-10-2 → D-8-4 → F-2/F-5).

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who are studying
in Korea — or want to launch their startup through Korea's immigration
system.

## Face 1: Student visa (D-2)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Default** | D-2 covers **study only** — any paid work requires a prior **part-time work permit** (시간제취업 허가; HiKorea online applications only since Aug 2025). Working even one hour without it = illegal employment → fines, visa cancellation, deportation risk |
| **Eligibility** | GPA ≥ 2.0 previous semester + Korean/English thresholds (TOPIK 3–4 or KIIP equivalent, tiered by degree level) |
| **Hours** | Standard cap **≤ 20 h/week Mon–Fri** during semester; weekends and vacations unrestricted. Universities apply tiered tables in practice (≈10 h/week without TOPIK up to 30–35 h/week with TOPIK 4 for graduate students) |
| **Job rules** | Max **2 workplaces** (D-2); simple part-time work only — no manufacturing/construction (TOPIK 4 exception), no dispatch/subcontract roles, no long-distance commutes; new permit per employer; validity ≤ 1 year |
| **Self-employment / your LLC** | ❌ The permit covers **part-time employment**. Operating your own business requires D-8/D-9 status — not a side permission on D-2 |
| **LLC posture** | Fully **passive** ([`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) |

## Face 2: Founder pipeline (OASIS — Global Startup Immigration Center)

| Stage | Visa | What it takes |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **1. Prepare** | **D-10-2** (startup preparation) | Complete OASIS orientation (Level 4+); 6-month extensions up to **2 years** while incorporating |
| **2. Launch** | **D-8-4** (technology/business startup) | Degree (associate+ in Korea or bachelor's+ abroad) + **OASIS score ≥ 60/300** + IP application/tech capability; Korean corporation + business registration |
| **3. Settle** | **F-2** (point-based resident) | After D-8 tenure + points (KIIP helps) |
| **4. Permanent** | **F-5** | Tech-startup route: 3 years on D-8 with ≥ **KRW 300M** invested and **≥ 2 Korean employees**; D-8-4 holders can convert directly when requirements are met |

K-Startup Grand Challenge participation adds points/recognition. A
**Korean entity + office** is required — the pass is tied to it.

## Tax note

Korea taxes residents on **worldwide income**, and its **CFC rules** can
attribute income of foreign entities controlled by Korean residents — a
Wyoming LLC actively managed from Seoul is squarely in scope. Same
effective-management logic as Germany/Japan.

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current Hi Korea /
MOJ / NTA guidance (hikorea.go.kr, oasisvisa.com) before acting.**
