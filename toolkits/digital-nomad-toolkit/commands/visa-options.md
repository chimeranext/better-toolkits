# /digital-nomad:visa-options

Guide to visa options for digital nomads who want to work from the US.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who want to work from the US.

## Visa options

| Visa | Best for | Requirements | Duration |
|------|----------|--------------|----------|
| **B-1** | Business visits, meetings | Proof of business, ties to home country | Up to 6 months |
| **E-2** | Treaty country investors | Treaty country nationality, substantial investment | Up to 5 years |
| **H-1B** | Specialty occupations | Bachelor's degree, employer sponsorship | Up to 6 years |
| **O-1** | Extraordinary ability | Evidence of extraordinary ability | Up to 3 years |
| **ESTA** | Short business visits | Visa Waiver Program country | Up to 90 days |

## E-2 Treaty Investor Visa

**Best for**: Citizens of treaty countries who want to invest in and operate a US business.

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Nationality** | Must be from a treaty country |
| **Investment** | Substantial investment (typically $100K+) |
| **Operations** | Must be actively operating, not just investing |
| **Control** | Must own 50%+ of the business |

**Treaty countries**: Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico, Spain, and many others.

## B-1 Business Visitor Visa

**Best for**: Short business visits, meetings, conferences.

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Business activities (meetings, negotiations) |
| **Ties to home country** | Proof of residence, job, family |
| **Duration** | Up to 6 months per visit |

## ESTA (Visa Waiver Program)

**Best for**: Short business visits from VWP countries.

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Nationality** | VWP country (most of Europe, Japan, South Korea, etc.) |
| **Duration** | Up to 90 days per visit |
| **No work** | Cannot work, only business activities |

## Digital nomad visas (outside the US)

If you want to work from outside the US, consider these digital nomad visas:

| Country | Visa | Requirements |
|---------|------|--------------|
| **Portugal** | D7 | Passive income or remote work |
| **Spain** | Digital Nomad Visa | Remote work for non-Spanish companies |
| **Costa Rica** | Digital Nomad Visa | $3,000/month income |
| **Mexico** | Temporary Resident | $2,000/month income |
| **Colombia** | Digital Nomad Visa | $700/month income |
