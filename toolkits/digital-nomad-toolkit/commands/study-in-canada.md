# /digital-nomad:study-in-canada

Keep your US Single-Member LLC alive while studying in Canada — where the
study permit grants **no work permit**.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who hold — or are
applying for — a Canadian study permit.

## The core rule

| Topic | Rule under a Canadian study permit |
|-------|------------------------------------|
| **Work authorization** | A study permit is **not** a work permit. Off-campus work and self-employment are not authorized by default |
| **Allowed work** | **On-campus only**, up to **~20 h/week** while classes are in session (full-time in scheduled breaks) |
| **Your LLC** | Invoicing US clients from Canadian soil is still **work performed in Canada** — the Wyoming entity and Stripe/Mercury flow do not change that analysis |
| **Why it matters** | Unauthorized work can jeopardize the study permit, any future PGWP application, and permanent-residence pathways |

> IRCC rules change frequently (hour caps and off-campus eligibility have
> been adjusted repeatedly). **Verify current IRCC policy before relying on
> any number here.**

## Tax exposure while in Canada

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Residency** | Enough days in Canada → tax resident (worldwide income); students are commonly caught by the residency tests |
| **CRA filing** | Report worldwide income on a Canadian return; foreign (US LLC) income included |
| **Double taxation** | Canada–US treaty + foreign tax credit (Form T2209) prevents most double taxation on the same income |
| **Foreign property** | If total cost of specified foreign properties (accounts, LLC interests) exceeds **CAD 100,000** → **Form T1135** |
| **US side** | Unchanged: keep federal/state filings via [`tax-compliance`](tax-compliance.md) |

## What is legal while studying

| Option | Status |
|--------|--------|
| On-campus employment (≤~20 h/week in session) | ✅ Authorized |
| Actively running the LLC from Canada (client calls, delivery, management) | ❌ Not authorized under the study permit |
| Keeping the LLC **passive** (registered agent, filings, banking hygiene, no services delivered from Canada) | ✅ Compatible — see [`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md) |
| Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) after finishing | ✅ The exit that re-enables active operation (PGWP is open work — employer/location unrestricted) |

**Recommended sequence**: study permit → LLC passive (maintenance only) →
PGWP (up to ~3 years, program-dependent) → resume active client work from
Canada, or return home / move on to [`visa-options`](visa-options.md).

## If you need income during the semester

- On-campus roles (TA, research assistant, campus jobs).
- Your home country's remote-work rules do not apply — physical performance governs, same as Germany.
- Deferring client work until PGWP is cheaper than a permit violation.

**Study-permit, work-permit, and residency rules change — verify current
IRCC and CRA guidance (or consult a licensed advisor) before acting.**
