# /digital-nomad:study-in-australia

Operate your US Single-Member LLC within the rules of an Australian student
visa (subclass 500) — where self-employment **is allowed**, but every active
hour counts against the work cap.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who hold — or are
applying for — an Australian student visa (subclass 500).

## The core rule

| Topic | Rule under subclass 500 |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Work while in session** | Up to **48 hours per fortnight** (conditions 8104/8105; a fortnight = 14 days starting Monday) — all work **combined** |
| **Work in scheduled breaks** | ✅ **Unlimited hours** |
| **Before the course starts** | ❌ No work (unless your previous visa allowed it) |
| **Research master's / doctorate** | Exempt from the cap once commenced |
| **Self-employment / your LLC** | ✅ **Allowed** — freelancing, ABN, company directorship count as "work"; there is no separate ban on business activity |
| **The catch** | Client calls, delivery, admin, marketing, invoicing — **every active hour counts toward the 48h cap**. An ABN adds no extra hours |

> The cap is measured **per fortnight**, not per week — 60 hours in one
> fortnight is a breach even if the fortnight after it is empty. Keep
> timesheets as evidence.

## What "running the LLC" looks like on a student visa

| Activity | Counts toward 48h/fortnight? |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Client work, calls, deliverables | ✅ Yes |
| Admin, marketing, bookkeeping, invoicing | ✅ Yes (it is work) |
| Passive ownership (no day-to-day involvement) | ❌ Not work — but must stay genuinely passive |
| Buying/holding investments | ❌ Not work |

**Risk beyond hours**: if Home Affairs concludes you are in Australia
**primarily to run a business** rather than study (Genuine Student
requirement, attendance, course progress), the visa — and future
applications — are at risk.

**Recommended sequence**: study → keep LLC hours inside the fortnight cap
(and zero during exam crunches) **or** keep it fully passive → after
graduation switch to the **Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)** for
unrestricted work.

## Tax exposure while in Australia

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Residence** | Residency tests (183-day pattern, domicile, intent) → taxed on **worldwide income** |
| **ABN/GST** | ABN is a tax identifier only — it grants **no** extra work rights; GST registration may apply above the turnover threshold |
| **Foreign company** | A foreign company **centrally managed and controlled** from Australia can be treated as Australian tax resident — same effective-management logic as Germany's §10 AO |
| **US side** | Unchanged: keep federal/state filings via [`tax-compliance`](tax-compliance.md) |

## The exit: Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

| Stream | Stay | Work rights |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Post-Higher Education** (degree+) | Usually **2–3 years** by qualification (coursework master's ≈2, research ≈3; +1–2 in regional areas) | **Unrestricted**, any sector |
| **Post-Vocational Education** (diploma/trade, MLTSSL occupation) | Up to 18 months | Unrestricted |
| Age at application | **≤ 35** (exceptions apply) | |
| Study requirement | ≥2 academic years (92 weeks) completed in ≤16 calendar months | |

## Study-financing facts (Australia)

- **Financial capacity** evidence required at visa application (living + tuition + travel — verify the current DHA figure).
- **OSHC** (Overseas Student Health Cover) mandatory for the whole visa duration.
- Student contribution/tuition varies by provider and stream.

**Visa conditions and work rules change — check your actual conditions in
VEVO and current Home Affairs/Study Australia guidance before acting.**
