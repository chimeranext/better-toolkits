# /digital-nomad:banking

Guide to opening a US bank account remotely as a digital nomad.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who need a US bank account.

## Remote banking options

| Bank | Requirements | Remote? | Best for |
|------|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Mercury** | EIN + Articles of Organization | Yes | Startups, freelancers |
| **Relay** | EIN + Operating Agreement | Yes | Small businesses |
| **Wise Business** | EIN + Proof of address | Yes | International transfers |
| **Brex** | EIN + Revenue | Yes | Startups with revenue |
| **Traditional banks** | EIN + Operating Agreement + Physical address | Sometimes | Established businesses |

## Requirements for remote banking

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **EIN** | From IRS |
| **Articles of Organization** | From state |
| **Operating Agreement** | Your LLC's rules |
| **Photo ID** | Passport, driver's license, or government ID |
| **Physical address** | Virtual address accepted by some banks |

## Recommended setup

1. **Primary**: Mercury or Relay (remote-friendly, no minimum balance)
2. **Secondary**: Wise Business (for international transfers, multi-currency)
3. **Optional**: Traditional bank (if you need a physical branch)
