# /digital-nomad:msa-b2b

Guide to billing international services as a US Single-Member LLC.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who bill international clients.

## How to bill as a US LLC

### Step 1 — Invoice your clients

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **From** | Your US LLC name + address |
| **To** | Client's company name + address |
| **Amount** | Agreed fee (USD or local currency) |
| **Payment method** | Wire transfer, PayPal, Wise, Stripe |

### Step 2 — Receive payment

| Method | Fees | Timeline |
|--------|------|----------|
| **Wire transfer** | $15-$45 | 1-3 business days |
| **Wise** | 0.5-1% | 1-2 business days |
| **PayPal** | 2.9% + $0.30 | Instant |
| **Stripe** | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2-7 business days |

### Step 3 — Record the transaction

- Keep invoices and receipts
- Record in your accounting system (QuickBooks, Wave, Xero)
- Separate business and personal expenses

## MSA B2B (Master Services Agreement)

If you're billing clients regularly, consider an MSA:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Scope** | Services you provide |
| **Pricing** | Rates and payment terms |
| **IP** | Who owns the work product |
| **Confidentiality** | NDA provisions |
| **Liability** | Limitation of liability |
| **Termination** | Notice period and transition |

## Tax implications

| Scenario | Tax implication |
|----------|-----------------|
| Client in US | Subject to US taxes |
| Client outside US | May be non-taxable in the US |
| Tax treaty country | May reduce withholding |
