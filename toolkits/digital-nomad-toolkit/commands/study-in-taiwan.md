# /digital-nomad:study-in-taiwan

Two faces of Taiwan for LLC operators: the **student route** (ARC + WDA
part-time work permit — 20 h/week in term, unlimited in breaks) and the
**founder routes** (Digital Nomad Visa, Employment Gold Card, Entrepreneur
Visa + company registration).

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who are applying
to study in Taiwan — or want to base themselves in Taipei (or the
Taipei–Hsinchu corridor) to build their business.

## Face 1: Student visa (ARC + work permit)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Status** | Degree students get an **ARC** (residence certificate); then a **part-time work permit** via the Workforce Development Agency online system (usually through your school) |
| **Part-time work** | **20 h/week** during the semester, **unlimited** during summer and winter breaks (Employment Services Act exception for international students). Working without a permit: fine **NTD 30k–150k**; employers face heavier fines |
| **Internship** | Post-graduation internships via your university: bachelor's with ≥ 70 average or master's+, up to **1 year** |
| **Self-employment / managing your LLC** | ❌ **Not covered** — the permit authorizes part-time *employment*, not running a business. Keep the LLC **passive** ([`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) |
| **Tax risk** | Taiwan taxes residents on **worldwide income** (> 183 days); Taiwan's **CFC rules (since 2023)** look through foreign entities you control — a disregarded US LLC with US-source income usually isn't hit directly, but model it with an advisor |

## Face 2: Founder routes

### Digital Nomad Visa — the LLC-native option

New via the National Development Council: **multiple-entry, 6 months,
extendable to a 2-year total stay**. For visa-exempt nationals (US
included), age 20+, proving **US$40k** annual salary/income (age ≥ 30) or
**US$20k** (age 20–29) in either of the last 2 years. The work must be for a
foreign employer **or your own foreign company** — i.e. your US LLC — and not
for Taiwanese clients. Apply at an overseas mission or (in Taiwan) at BOCA
before your current stay expires.

### Employment Gold Card — the all-in-one

Resident visa + open work permit + ARC + re-entry permit in one card
(Foreign Professional Act Art. 8): **no employer needed**, validity 1–3
years, across 12 professional fields (science & technology via NSTC, digital
via MoDA, etc.). The strongest base for a founder who qualifies — and
Gold-Card time counts toward permanent-residence points.

### Entrepreneur Visa

No company needed to apply: get **MOEA qualification** via one of the
recognized routes (VC/NDF investment or approved crowdfunding ≥ NTD 2M;
incubator/park residency such as Startup Terrace with recommendation;
notable patents or professional expertise; competition awards; or ≥ NTD 1M
invested in an innovative startup where you act as legal representative,
manager or director). Initial **≤ 2 years**; each extension requires real
traction: **NTD 3M revenue, or NTD 1M operating expenses, or ≥ 3 full-time
Taiwanese employees** (or an equivalent contribution). Qualifying Gold Card
holders can convert.

### Company setup (subsidiary of your US LLC)

Name reservation → **MOEA foreign-investment approval** (Department of
Investment Review) → remit capital → capital verification → company
registration at the city/county (if paid-in capital < NTD 500M) → tax
registration. Tax: **CIT 20% + 5% surtax on undistributed earnings**;
dividends to non-residents **21% WHT** — ⚠️ **no US–Taiwan income tax treaty
is in force** (the 1984 agreement covers shipping/air income only; the
US–Taiwan double-tax relief bill passed the US House in January 2025 but
remains pending in the Senate — do not model on it).

### Ecosystem anchor

Taiwan ranked **#20 globally** in StartupBlink 2026 (first time in the top
20; Hardware & IoT #7) — Taipei–Hsinchu–Miaoli is its own ranked region.
**COMPUTEX / InnoVEX 2027: June 1–4, TaiNEX 2** (exhibitors: tech startups
founded on/after 1 Jan 2019; early-bird registration until 31 Jan 2027) is
the flagship hardware/startup stage. Startup Genome puts Taipei at $12B
ecosystem value. Semiconductor and hardware depth is unmatched; consumer
market and capital are smaller than Shenzhen's.

## Recommended sequence

Study (ARC + 20 h/week permit, LLC passive) → **Digital Nomad Visa** (2-year
runway on your own LLC) or **Gold Card** after graduation → **Entrepreneur
Visa + company** once you want local substance. Alternative: Digital Nomad
Visa first to validate in Taipei, then register the Taiwan company.

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current NIA/MOEA
guidance (immigration.gov.tw, moea.gov.tw) and Taiwanese tax advice before
acting.**
