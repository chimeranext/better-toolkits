# /digital-nomad:study-in-singapore

Two faces of Singapore for LLC operators: the **Student Pass** (tightly
restricted work) and the **founder passes** (EntrePass, Tech.Pass — all tied
to a Singapore company, not your Wyoming LLC).

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who are studying
in Singapore — or want to base their startup there.

## Face 1: Student Pass (ICA)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Work exemption** | Only for full-time students at **listed institutions** (MOE/government-aided, ICA-listed schools). Exchange students: **no work at all**. Private-institution students: generally need MOM approval |
| **During term** | **≤ 16 h/week**, or a curriculum internship/industrial attachment (compulsory or elective) |
| **During vacation** | Unlimited hours (still within the exemption list) |
| **Self-employment / your LLC** | ❌ The exemption covers **employment**, not running a business. Working without a required pass is a criminal offence → pass revocation, fines, entry ban |
| **LLC posture** | Fully **passive** ([`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) |

## Face 2: Founder passes (MOM / EDB)

Both require a **Singapore-incorporated company** — the pass is anchored to
an ACRA Pte Ltd, not to a foreign LLC. A Wyoming LLC can sit alongside (e.g.,
as a client-revenue vehicle) but it cannot sponsor the pass.

### EntrePass

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Entity** | ACRA-registered **Pte Ltd**; you hold **≥ 30%** of it; venture-backed or owns innovative technology |
| **One qualifying criterion** | ≥ **S$100,000** raised in a single round from eligible investors · **or** support from a government-recognised / internationally renowned incubator or accelerator (e.g., Y Combinator) · **or** you founded and sold a venture-backed/tech business · **or** registered IP providing competitive advantage · **or** an ongoing research collaboration with an IHL / A*STAR / CREATE institution |
| **Not eligible** | F&B (coffee shops, hawker, bars), massage/reflexology, TCM, employment agencies, geomancy |
| **Validity / renewal** | 1 year → 1 year → 2 years; renewal tracks **local hiring and business spending**. Companies > 12 months old are assessed on renewal criteria |

### Tech.Pass — being replaced from 28 January 2027

Established founders/leaders: last drawn fixed monthly salary **≥ S$22,500**
plus ≥ 5 cumulative years in a leading role at a tech company valued
**≥ US$500M** or that raised **≥ US$30M**. 2 + 2 years; multiple concurrent
roles allowed (start companies, employ, consult, direct).

> **Sunset**: from **28 January 2027** MOM/EDB stop accepting new and renewal
> Tech.Pass applications — replaced by the new **ONE Pass (AI and Tech)**
> track. Check EDB terms before planning around Tech.Pass. EDB's **Global
> Founder Programme** is the current founder-facing umbrella.

## Tax note

Singapore taxes at a headline **17%** on Singapore-sourced/derived income
with a territorial framing — and a foreign company **managed and controlled**
from Singapore can be pulled into net. Keep US filings clean via
[`tax-compliance`](tax-compliance.md).

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current MOM/ICA/EDB/
IRAS guidance before acting.**
