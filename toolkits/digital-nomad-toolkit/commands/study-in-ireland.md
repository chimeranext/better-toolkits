# /digital-nomad:study-in-ireland

Keep your US Single-Member LLC alive while studying in Ireland on a Stamp 2
student permission — where students **must not engage in any business or
trade**.

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who hold — or are
applying for — an Irish non-EEA student permission (Stamp 2, ILEP course).

## The core rule

| Topic | Rule under Stamp 2 (student permission) |
|-------|-----------------------------------------|
| **Casual employment** | ✅ Up to **20 h/week** in term; **40 h/week** in the fixed vacations (June–September and 15 December–15 January) |
| **Business or trade** | ❌ Explicit condition: *"You must not engage in any other business or trade"* — running your LLC actively from Ireland is out |
| **Self-employment in placements** | Not allowed even in internships: *"employment cannot be in a self-employed capacity"* |
| **Stamp 2A** (non-ILEP course) | ❌ **No work at all** |
| **Multiple jobs** | The 20 h cap is a **total**, not per employer |
| **Your LLC** | Invoicing US clients from Irish soil is business activity performed in Ireland — the Wyoming entity and Stripe/Mercury flow do not change that analysis |

## Getting permission that *does* allow business

| Route | What it gives you |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Stamp 1 + ISD letter** | Permission to work/operate a business **only if** the ISD letter explicitly says so (employment permit or named permission) |
| **Critical Skills / General Employment Permit** | Employer-sponsored route → Stamp 1; not a student route |
| **Start-Up Entrepreneur Programme** | Innovative business + **≥ €50,000** funding; not retail/catering/personal services |
| **Third Level Graduate (Stamp 1G)** | Post-study: **12 months** (Level 8) or **24 months** (Level 9+, master's/PhD, renewed at 12 months on job-seeking evidence) — work without a permit, but **self-employment is not allowed** under the programme |

**Recommended sequence**: student visa first → keep the LLC passive
(maintenance only, see [`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) →
either a Critical Skills permit post-graduation (Stamp 1G → Stamp 1 with
business letter) or return home / relocate to a nomad-friendly jurisdiction
before resuming active client work.

## Tax exposure while in Ireland

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Residence** | 183+ days (or part-year pattern) → Irish tax residence → **worldwide income** taxed |
| **Effective management** | Managing the LLC from Ireland can draw Revenue attention to where the company is really managed — same §10-AO-style logic as Germany |
| **US side** | Unchanged: keep federal/state filings via [`tax-compliance`](tax-compliance.md) |

## Requirements when applying

- Full-time course on the **ILEP** (Interim List of Eligible Programmes) — anything else gets Stamp 2A (no work).
- **Proof of funds** to support your stay *without relying on casual work* — checked at visa/registration (verify current ISD thresholds).
- Annual renewal with progression; maximum **7 years** of student permission overall.

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current ISD/Revenue
practice (irishimmigration.ie, citizensinformation.ie) before acting.**
