# /digital-nomad:study-in-japan

Two faces of Japan for LLC operators: the **student visa** (study first, LLC
passive) and the **founder routes** (Startup Visa → Business Manager).

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who are applying
to study in Japan — or want to move to Japan to build their business.

## Face 1: Student visa (留学)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Part-time work** | Allowed with *Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than Permitted* (資格外活動許可): **28 h/week** (8 h/day in long vacations); no adult entertainment |
| **Self-employment / managing your LLC** | ❌ **Not covered by the permission** — it authorizes part-time *employment* only. Managing a company (even your own foreign LLC) is unauthorized activity; a separate side-business permission for managers is, in practice, not granted |
| **LLC posture** | Fully **passive**: registrations, filings, zero-activity months only ([`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) |
| **Tax risk** | A foreign company whose **effective management** sits in Japan can be treated as a **Japanese corporation** for tax — same logic as Germany's §10 AO. Client calls, hiring, and contract signing from Tokyo all point that way |

## Face 2: Founder routes

### Startup Visa (Designated Activities, ≤ 2 years)

The on-ramp that **avoids** meeting Business Manager requirements on day one:

1. Submit a **Business Preparation Activity Plan** to a certified Foreign
   Entrepreneurship Promotion Organization.
2. Receive a Certificate of Confirmation → **Designated Activities** residence
   (nationwide since January 2025, with a grace period to meet the office and
   business-scale requirements).
3. Fulfil Business Manager requirements inside Japan → change status.

### Business Manager visa (経営・管理)

Tightened by the October 2025 amendment — **all** of these now apply:

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Capital / investment** | **≥ ¥30 million**, with strict source-of-funds evidence (student part-time earnings are **not** accepted as capital; parental funds need remittance records) |
| **Employee** | ≥ 1 full-time employee who is a Japanese national / PR / spouse-type status (30+ h/week, direct contract, social insurance enrolled) |
| **Office** | Real premises in Japan — **virtual and shared offices are rejected**; residence-as-office only under strict conditions (signage, dedicated room, landlord consent) |
| **Business plan** | Verified by a qualified professional (new in the 2025 amendment) |
| **Experience / degree** | ≥ 3 years management experience **or** a master's/doctorate |
| **Japanese** | Language proficiency evidence required |
| **Side jobs** | ❌ Not allowed on this visa itself |

### J-Find (Designated Activities — Future Creation Talent)

Graduates (last 5 years) of universities ranked top-100 in ≥ 2 global
rankings: up to **2 years** of job-hunting/startup preparation with
**unrestricted part-time work** and only a savings proof (≈ ¥200k). A common
bridge from a Japanese master's into a startup without immediate ¥30M.

## Recommended sequence

Study (student visa, LLC passive) → **Startup Visa or J-Find** after
graduation → **Business Manager** once capital + employee + office are in
place. Alternative: degree at a top-100 university → J-Find → Business
Manager.

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current ISA/MOJ
guidance (isa.go.jp, jetro.go.jp) and Japanese tax advice before acting.**
