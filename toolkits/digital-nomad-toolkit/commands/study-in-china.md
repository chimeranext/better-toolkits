# /digital-nomad:study-in-china

Two faces of mainland China for LLC operators: the **student visa** (X1/X2
residence for study — no work permit, only a capped school-mediated
work-study endorsement) and the **founder routes** (WFOE subsidiary of your US
LLC + work permit A/B, or the new K visa for STEM talent).

## Audience

Freelancers and digital nomads with a US Single-Member LLC who are applying
to study in China — or want to move to Shenzhen/Qianhai to build their
business through a Chinese subsidiary.

## Face 1: Student visa (X1/X2)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Visa types** | **X1** (study > 180 days) → study-type residence permit; **X2** (≤ 180 days) → stay permit. **A work permit (Z) cannot be combined with study status** — they are mutually exclusive residence types |
| **Part-time work** | Off-campus work-study is possible **only via a residence-permit endorsement** (加注): school approval → PSB exit-entry endorsement of employer, location and duration ([Entry-Exit Regulations Art. 22](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202508/content_7036550.htm)). Caps (MOE work-study measures): **8 h/week, 40 h/month** in term; **16 h/week, 80 h/month** in vacations. Conditions: 18+, valid study permit with ≥ 6 months left, good standing, insurance, employer registered with the local exit-entry bureau |
| **Internship** | Internship endorsement follows the same school + PSB route and must relate to your field of study. X2 stay permits **cannot** carry an internship endorsement — only study residence permits and private-affairs visas ([Shenzhen PSB](https://www.sz.gov.cn/hdjl/ywzsk/gaj/crj/content/post_12426976.html)) |
| **Self-employment / managing your LLC** | ❌ **Not covered** — the endorsement authorizes school-mediated part-time work or a study-related internship, not business management. Running your own company on a study permit is unauthorized activity: fines (per day of illegal work), detention, deportation |
| **LLC posture** | Fully **passive**: registrations, filings, zero-activity months only ([`maintain-llc-abroad`](maintain-llc-abroad.md)) |
| **Tax risk** | A foreign company whose **effective management** sits in China can be treated as a **Chinese tax resident** on worldwide income — client calls, hiring, and contract signing from Shenzhen all point that way |

## Face 2: Founder routes

### WFOE subsidiary of your US LLC

Standard and well-trodden under the Foreign Investment Law 2020 + Company Law
2023. Shenzhen/Qianhai is the friendliest registration path:

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Negative list** | Check the national or **Qianhai FTZ negative list** first — most services/tech sectors are open |
| **Registration** | AMR Shenzhen (Qianhai has a dedicated FIE track with affiliate-address hosting) → **business license** → foreign-investment information report (initial) |
| **Banking / SAFE** | Basic CNY account + FX account → SAFE foreign-exchange registration for capital injection |
| **Capital** | Subscribed capital, **paid in within 5 years** (2023 Company Law; existing FIEs until 30 Jun 2027). No universal minimum, but the amount must match your business plan |
| **US documents** | Certificate of good standing etc. with an **apostille** — China acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention on 7 Nov 2023, so consular legalization is no longer needed |

### Work permit A/B → Z visa → work residence permit

Once the WFOE exists it can employ **you**:

| Tier | Who | Shenzhen bar (2025) |
|------|-----|---------------------|
| **A** | High-end talent incl. recognized "international entrepreneurs" | Salary ≥ 6× Shenzhen average wage (≈ ¥55k/month) |
| **B** | Degree + 2 years' experience, < 60 years old | Bachelor's + salary ≥ 4× average wage (≈ ¥37k/month) |
| **C** | Quota-based fallback | Labor-market test applies |

Chain: WFOE applies via the STIC work-permit system → work-permit certificate →
**Z visa** at a Chinese mission → work residence permit within 30 days of entry.
High-tier holders can get residence up to 5 years.

### K visa (new since 1 Oct 2025)

For **young foreign STEM talent** (bachelor's+ from well-known
universities/research institutions, or STEM faculty/researchers): no Chinese
employer or inviter required, more generous entries/validity/stay than other
ordinary visas, and after entry you may conduct **exchange, entrepreneurship
and business activities** ([State Council Q&A](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202508/content_7036550.htm)).
A fast on-ramp for a technical founder — but long-term operation still runs
through the WFOE + work-permit chain above.

### Ecosystem anchor

Shenzhen is a hardware/robotics/AI cluster: ~$1T combined ecosystem value,
Tencent, BYD, 49 unicorns (Dealroom); **#1 worldwide R&D-engine ranking** in
Startup Genome GSER. Qianhai offers FTZ registration, hosting addresses and a
15% CIT rate for encouraged industries. Mainland China has **no US tax
treaty**, so dividends from the WFOE to a US parent face 10% WHT (US-China
treaty) only when the US parent qualifies as beneficial owner — a
disregarded LLC usually doesn't; a check-the-box election changes the whole
US-side picture (GILTI/Subpart F, Form 5471). Get a cross-border CPA before
incorporating, not after.

## Recommended sequence

Study (X1, LLC passive, work-study endorsement only) → graduation → **WFOE +
work permit A/B** (or **K visa** entry for STEM profiles) → work residence
permit. Alternative: land in Shenzhen on business/other status, register the
WFOE first, then employ yourself.

**Immigration and tax rules change frequently — verify current NIA/STIC
guidance (nia.gov.cn, stic.sz.gov.cn) and Chinese tax advice before acting.**
