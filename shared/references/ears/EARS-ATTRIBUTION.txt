EARS (Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax) materials — attribution

`scripts/ears-lint.py` is adapted from the `ears-requirements` skill's
`validate_ears.py` (OpenAI / community skill packaging). Pattern definitions
follow the public EARS literature (Mavin et al., INCOSE) and the pattern
templates commonly published with that skill.

Spanish obligation forms (`debe`) are original to better-toolkits so Spanish
customer-facing SRS and bilingual issue AC can share one gate.

Related practice notes (WHEN / THEN / SHALL CONTINUE TO for unchanged
behavior) align with public Specs guidance such as
https://kiro.dev/docs/specs/best-practices/ — not a redistribution of that site.

This pack does not claim INCOSE certification.
