# Digital Nomad

The toolkit for freelancers and digital nomads who want to operate as a US
Single-Member LLC without jumping to a startup or shared services:

```
Form LLC → Tax compliance → Remote banking → International billing → Visa options → Study / found abroad → Route decision
```

This is a **playbook**: it explains *when and why* to use each skill. For the
executable steps, open the skill itself (the runbook).

## When to use this toolkit

Use Digital Nomad when the work is your own independent operation:

- You want to stay as a single-member LLC, not incorporate a startup.
- You need to bill international clients in USD.
- You want to keep taxes clean in each country you live in.
- You work remotely from anywhere in the world.
- You plan to study or found abroad while keeping (or pausing) your LLC.

## Core skills — when and why

### Foundation

| Skill | When to use it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **entity-formation** | you are setting up your business identity | it forms a single-member LLC with a virtual address and registered agent |
| **tax-compliance** | you must keep taxes clean | it covers non-resident taxes, annual reporting, and tax treaties |
| **banking** | you need a remote bank account | it walks opening accounts with Mercury, Relay, or Wise |
| **msa-b2b** | you bill international clients | it shows how to bill services as an LLC |
| **visa-options** | you need to stay abroad legally | it maps digital nomad visa options: B-1, E-2, H-1B |
| **maintain-llc-abroad** | you live abroad and must keep the LLC alive | it keeps the single-member LLC in good standing from a student or founder address |

## When NOT to use this toolkit

- For legal, tax, or financial advice — this toolkit is educational only; consult a lawyer and accountant.
- For forming a venture-backed startup — use the Startup Lifecycle toolkit.
- For portfolio-level decisions across multiple ventures — use the Venture Studio toolkit.
- For contract and delivery workflows between a fractional CTO and a client — use the Fractional CTO toolkit.
