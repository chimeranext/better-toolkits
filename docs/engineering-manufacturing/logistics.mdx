# Logistics

The Logistics toolkit covers supply chain and importing for companies bringing
goods into Costa Rica or other LATAM markets: procurement, customs regimes,
INCOTERMS, trade agreements, and inventory management. Use it when you plan or
run import and supply-chain operations.

This is a **playbook**: it explains *when and why* to use each skill. For the
executable steps, open the skill itself (the runbook).

## When to use this toolkit

Use Logistics when the work is moving and tracking goods:

- You plan imports or supply-chain operations.
- You categorize suppliers or run a procure-to-pay process.
- You navigate customs regimes and import documents.
- You decide between FOB and CIF or other INCOTERMS.
- You manage inventory and forecasting.
- You want agent-searchable local documentation from a Notion library.

## Core skills — when and why

| Skill | When to use it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **logistics-supply-chain** | you plan imports or supply-chain operations | it gives procurement, customs, INCOTERMS 2020, trade agreements, and inventory references |
| **notion-content-ingestion** | you have a Notion guide database and want local searchable docs | it crawls and enriches content into versioned Markdown |
| **deep-research** | you need researched, structured content for training or documentation | it combines web and crawled sources into organized Markdown chapters |

## When NOT to use this toolkit

- For physical manufacturing decisions, process selection, or tolerances — use the Manufacturing & Robotics toolkit.
- For CAD, modeling, or digital twins — use the 3D Graphics & CAD toolkit.
- For electrical or PCB design — use the Schematics & PCB toolkit.
- For legal or tax advice on a specific import — consult a specialist rather than this toolkit.
