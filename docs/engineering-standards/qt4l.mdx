# QA Traceability (four layers)

**Scope:** Req → automated test → manual QA → HITL

## When to apply it

You are tracing requirements to test coverage.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/qa-traceability-four-layers.md` in the repository.
