# Stop-on-Anomaly

**Scope:** Stop on any anomaly; record the owning boundary and a root-cause hypothesis before continuing. Never "see if the rest works" after a failure.

## When to apply it

You hit an anomaly or a failure.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/jidoka.md` in the repository.
