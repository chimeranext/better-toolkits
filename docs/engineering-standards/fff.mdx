# Feature Flag Families

**Scope:** Two-level model: families (Release, Experimentation, Ops, Access, Decommissioning, Security vendorization) each with an internal taxonomy of subtypes. Interfaces with REL/MPH/GGT/DDD.

## When to apply it

You are adding or managing a feature flag.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/feature-flag-families.md` in the repository.
