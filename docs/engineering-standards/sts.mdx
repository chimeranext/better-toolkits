# Stderr/Stream Standards

**Scope:** Never send a stream to null; fold stderr into the same sink (`> out.log 2>&1` or `2>&1 \

## When to apply it

You are capturing command output or logging.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/sts.md` in the repository.
