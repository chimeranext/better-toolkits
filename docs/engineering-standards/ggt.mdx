# GitFlow + Trunk-based

**Scope:** Hybrid model: feature→`develop` rebase. `develop`→`main` merge commit. Auditable setup + repo-initial-setup step

## When to apply it

You are taking a branch to a shared or production branch.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/gitflow-trunk.md` in the repository.
