# Test Assertion Standards

**Scope:** Aserciones de contrato e invariantes, no snapshots: nunca conteos literales de registros que crecen; derivar del source of truth (p.ej. `NPM_PACKAGES.length`).

## When to apply it

You are writing test assertions.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/test-assertion-standards.md` in the repository.
