# Repository Drift Prevention

**Scope:** Single source of truth per artifact; detect and prevent drift across repos/config

## When to apply it

You are managing a shared artifact or configuration.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/repository-drift-prevention.md` in the repository.
