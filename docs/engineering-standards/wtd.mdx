# Worktree Discipline

**Scope:** Worktrees are transient and never committed. Parity (1 issue = 1 worktree = 1 PR) is an association policy, not persistence.

## When to apply it

You are creating a worktree.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/worktree-discipline.md` in the repository.
