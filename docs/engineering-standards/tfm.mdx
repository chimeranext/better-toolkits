# Formal Model TLA+

**Scope:** Standardized TLA+/TLC modeling and validation recipe

## When to apply it

You are modeling a critical invariant or a concurrent system.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/formal-model-tla.md` in the repository.
