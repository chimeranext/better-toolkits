# Release Cut

**Scope:** One consolidated `develop`→`main` PR. CTO owns the QA audit (QT4L). A second human reviewer is the sole PROD gate. Hotfixes only via `hotfix/*`.

## When to apply it

You are cutting a release.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/release-cut.md` in the repository.
