# Anti-Fragility via Fault Injection

**Scope:** Harden every layer by injecting faults (mutation, formal self-test, chaos, gate negatives). "Never seen fail" is not a pass.

## When to apply it

You are hardening a layer or a component.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/anti-fragility-fault-injection.md` in the repository.
