# Build-Artifact Gitignore

**Scope:** Every repo vendors the ignore patterns from `vendored-gitignore.toml` (venv, node_modules, target, build, caches). Promotes the symlink build-artifact policy

## When to apply it

You are handling build artifacts or .gitignore.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/build-artifacts-gitignore.md` in the repository.
