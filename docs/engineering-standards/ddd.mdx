# Domain-Driven Design

**Scope:** Bounded contexts, ubiquitous language, aggregates; design guided by the domain

## When to apply it

You are designing around a business domain.

## Why

The standard keeps the work consistent, reviewable, and traceable across the
project. Applying it at the right time avoids rework and keeps the codebase
healthy.

## Procedure

The detailed procedure, commands, and enforcement for this standard live in
`shared/references/engineering-standards/domain-driven-design.md` in the repository.
