# Business Model

The business model toolkit guides you through the full startup lifecycle, from
problem validation through solution design, execution, and investor pitch. Use
it when you must turn an idea into a validated, structured business model.

This is a **playbook**: it explains *when and why* to use each skill. For the
executable steps, open the skill itself (the runbook).

## When to use this toolkit

Use the business model toolkit when the work is business definition and
validation:

- You must validate a problem before building a solution.
- You need a business model canvas, unit economics, or a financial model.
- You must define a brand identity or go-to-market plan.
- You must prepare an investor pitch.

It is the default toolkit for taking an idea from hypothesis to a pitchable
business.

## Core skills — when and why

| Skill | When to use it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **problem-validation** | you are starting and need to validate the problem, founder profile, customer, and market | it runs the problem phase with customer forces and market research gates |
| **customer-interview-system** | you need to interview customers | it gives scripts, silence rules, and pattern detection to separate real from aspirational answers |
| **solution-design** | the problem is validated and you need a solution | it builds the business model canvas and designs the MVP experiment |
| **execution-plan** | you must define revenue, unit economics, OKRs, and a KPI dashboard | it establishes the financial and operational foundation |
| **brand-identity** | you need to define positioning, tone, and brand essence | it builds the brand steering wheel and emotional map |
| **growth-acceleration** | you must plan go-to-market, roadmap, hiring, and advisory | it covers the growth and team phases before the pitch |
| **pitch-deck** | you need an investor presentation | it synthesizes prior work into a structured deck |
| **pitch-script** | you need a short recorded video pitch | it writes a teleprompter-style script for a 3-minute pitch |
| **product-vision-audit** | a product exists and you must compare vision to implementation | it audits the codebase against the declared vision and flags gaps |
| **landing-page-design** | the solution is set and you need a conversion page | it guides high-conversion landing page design and CRO |

## When NOT to use this toolkit

- For executing paid media or revenue campaigns — use the revenue-growth toolkit.
- For shipping an app to stores — use the app go-to-market release toolkit.
- For user research maps and interviews — use the UX research toolkit.
- For course or learning design — use the instructional design toolkit.
- A single quick question does not need the full lifecycle.
